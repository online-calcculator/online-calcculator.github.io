How to Use the Auto Lease Calculator

Choose Total Price to estimate a monthly lease from the vehicle price, or Monthly Payment to find the vehicle price that fits a target payment. Enter the lease length in months, interest rate or money factor, down payment, trade-in credit, sales tax, and residual value. Click Calculate to see the breakdown above the form; results update on the same page.

Understanding the Inputs

  • Auto Price: The negotiated vehicle price, also known as the gross capitalized cost in this simplified calculation.
  • Money Factor / Interest Rate: The lease financing rate. Change the dropdown to convert between a money factor and APR equivalent.
  • Down Payment and Trade-in: Amounts reducing the price used to calculate lease payments. Trade-in credit assumes no outstanding vehicle loan.
  • Residual Value: The vehicle's estimated value at the end of the lease. Enter the dollar amount, not a percentage.
  • Sales Tax: An illustrative rate. The actual taxable base and timing depend on state and local law.

Auto Lease Payment Formula

Let P be the negotiated price, D the cash down payment, T the trade-in credit, R the residual value, n the months, f the money factor, and t the sales-tax rate in decimal form:

Adjusted capitalized cost: C = P − D − T

Monthly depreciation: (C − R) ÷ n

Monthly financing (rent) charge: (C + R) × f

Monthly lease payment with tax: [(C − R) ÷ n + (C + R) × f] × (1 + t)

Approximate APR equivalent: money factor × 2,400, expressed as a percentage

To calculate auto price from monthly payment, the calculator rearranges the same equation: C = [M ÷ (1 + t) + R × (1/n − f)] ÷ (1/n + f), then P = C + D + T, where M is the entered tax-inclusive monthly payment.

Worked Example

With a $50,000 vehicle, $10,000 down, no trade-in, a $24,000 residual, a 36-month term, a 0.00208 money factor, and 7% tax, the adjusted capitalized cost is $40,000. Depreciation is $444.44 per month, financing is $133.12, and monthly tax is $40.43. The estimated monthly lease payment is $617.99; all 36 payments sum to approximately $22,247.78.

Understanding the Results

Total Cost to Own After Lease Ends assumes all lease payments are made and you then purchase the vehicle for its residual value. It also adds the initial down payment, tax on that down payment, and estimated tax on the buyout. The purchase comparison uses an amortizing loan for the same term at the money-factor-equivalent APR, with purchase sales tax paid upfront. These are scenario estimates, not formal contract disclosures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a money factor the same as APR?

No. The money factor is commonly used for automotive leases. Multiplying the money factor by 2,400 gives a conventional APR comparison figure, not necessarily the exact financing cost in a specific lease agreement.

Can I enter APR instead of the money factor?

Yes. Choose Interest Rate(%) from the dropdown. The calculator converts APR percent to a money factor by dividing by 2,400, while preserving the value when switching back.

Is sales tax always applied to the monthly lease payment?

No. Some jurisdictions tax lease payments, while others tax a vehicle's price, the entire lease, or other components upfront. The model follows the reference example and assumes monthly payment tax, plus tax on the down payment and optional lease-end buyout.

Are title, registration, mileage, and other fees included?

No. Acquisition fees, disposition fees, security deposits, registration, title, maintenance, excess wear, and mileage charges are excluded. Request an itemized quote before entering a lease.
