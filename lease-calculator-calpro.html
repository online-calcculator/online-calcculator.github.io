How to Use the Lease Calculator

Select Fixed Rate to estimate the payment from an annual interest rate, or Fixed Pay to find the interest rate implied by a known monthly payment. Enter the starting asset value, the estimated value remaining at lease end, and a term in years and months. Click Calculate to update the results and the principal-versus-interest doughnut chart.

The Clear button empties the fields. Use the Save icon above the results to download a text summary. The calculator assumes payments made at the end of each month; it does not include upfront payments, taxes, maintenance, insurance, security deposits, or other fees.

Lease Payment Formula

The asset value equals the present value of the monthly payments plus the present value of the residual amount. The monthly payment is therefore:

PMT = [A − R / (1 + i)n] × i / [1 − (1 + i)−n]

Where A is the asset value, R is residual value, i is the annual nominal interest rate divided by 12 (in decimal form), and n is the number of months. With a 0% interest rate: PMT = (A − R) / n.

In Fixed Pay mode, the same present-value equation is solved numerically for the nonnegative monthly rate, which is multiplied by 12 to report the nominal annual interest rate. This is not a fee-inclusive legal APR disclosure. A supplied payment below the 0%-interest payment cannot produce a nonnegative rate; the calculator reports an input error rather than inventing a result.

Worked Lease Example

An asset valued at $20,000, with $8,000 residual value, a 36-month term, and 6% nominal annual interest, produces a monthly payment of about $405.06. The unrounded payment over 36 months totals $14,582.28, including $12,000.00 of asset value used and $2,582.28 of interest. Displayed totals use the full-precision payment before rounding to cents.

Understanding the Results

Principal and residual value

The principal portion shown in the chart is the asset value minus the residual value. Residual value is the estimated value retained at the lease's end, not a fee included in the monthly payments. This simplified model is designed for a lease with a final residual amount.

Total interest

Total interest is the total of the monthly payments minus the difference between the asset value and residual value. With a typical depreciating asset, the chart shows these as principal and interest shares. If the breakdown contains negative shares, the chart is hidden rather than displaying misleading proportions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I enter a lease term entirely in months?

Yes. Enter 0 years and the full number of months. You can also use a fractional number of years, provided the combined term is a whole number of months. Terms between 1 and 1,200 months are accepted.

Why might my dealer quote be different?

A real lease quote may include acquisition charges, taxes, down payments, rebates, advance payments, add-ons, money-factor pricing, and a different treatment of residual value. Those items are not built into this general-purpose calculator.

Does the Fixed Pay rate include compound interest?

It solves the monthly rate of the lease equation and displays twelve times that monthly rate as a nominal annual percentage. An effective annual yield with monthly compounding would be (1 + i)12 − 1, which is a different figure.

What happens if the residual value exceeds the asset value?

The underlying equation can describe an appreciating asset, but it can produce atypical or negative monthly payments. The calculator rejects negative monthly payments and omits the standard principal/interest chart when its proportions would be misleading.
