    How to Use the Fuel Cost Calculator

    Enter the one-way or round-trip distance you plan to drive. Choose miles or kilometers, enter your vehicle’s fuel efficiency, and choose the unit shown by your dashboard or vehicle specifications. Finally, enter the price of fuel per U.S. gallon or per liter. Select Calculate to see the trip's fuel use and estimated cost above the form.

    Changing the fuel-efficiency unit changes the meaning of the numeric value. For example, 10 L/100 km is not the same as 10 mpg. Use the Clear button to empty the numeric fields and start a new calculation.

    Fuel Cost Formula

    The estimate is based on the amount of fuel consumed multiplied by the cost of each unit of fuel:

    Trip fuel cost = fuel used × fuel price per same volume unit.

    For a vehicle measured in liters per 100 kilometers, the fuel volume is (distance in km ÷ 100) × L/100 km. For miles per U.S. gallon, it is distance in miles ÷ mpg. For kilometers per liter, it is distance in km ÷ km/L. For liters per mile, it is distance in miles × L/mile.

    Worked Example: A 500 km Trip

    For a trip of 500 km, fuel efficiency of 10 L/100 km, and price of $3 per liter, fuel consumption is 500 ÷ 100 × 10 = 50 liters. The estimated total cost is 50 × $3 = $150. At 5 L/100 km, the same trip would consume 25 liters and cost $75.

    Understanding Your Result

    The result reports the estimated fuel quantity, total fuel expense, equivalent volume in the other unit system, and cost per mile or kilometer. A comparison list shows how fuel use and trip cost would change at other efficiency ratings. These calculations assume the entered fuel efficiency stays constant for the whole trip; hills, traffic, weather, idling, vehicle load, and driving habits may change actual consumption.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does this work for a round trip?

    Yes. Enter the full round-trip distance, or double the one-way distance before calculating.

    Are the gallons U.S. or imperial?

    All gallon calculations use U.S. liquid gallons, not imperial gallons. Convert imperial-gallon measurements before entering them as mpg.

    Can I use a different currency?

    The formula works with any currency, but this calculator labels results in dollars ($), like the reference. For clearly labeled totals, convert your price to dollars before entering it.

    Why might my actual cost differ?

    Real-world fuel economy varies with speed, route conditions, idling, tire pressure, climate controls, and traffic. Taxes or transaction charges may also affect the price you pay.
