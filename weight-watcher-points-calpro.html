How to Use the Weight Watcher Points Calculator

Choose the historical point system that matches the plan or recipe you are reviewing. Enter the nutrients from one food serving (not necessarily 100 g), choose the units shown on the nutrition label, and press Calculate. Your estimated result appears directly above the submitted form; other calculators remain available below. Select Clear to start again, or use Print / Save to print or save a PDF of the page.

Historical Points Formulas

SmartPoints (December 2015–November 2021)

Estimated points = max(0, round(0.0305 × kcal + 0.12 × sugar (g) + 0.275 × saturated fat (g) − 0.098 × protein (g))). This commonly published approximation is not an official WW formula. Since 2022, the program has used different inputs, including added sugars, fiber and unsaturated fat; this calculator does not reproduce that modern formula.

U.S. PointsPlus (November 2010–December 2015)

Estimated PointsPlus = max(0, round((16 × protein (g) + 19 × carbohydrate (g) + 45 × total fat (g) − 14 × fiber (g)) / 175)). This is a widely shared historical equation for the U.S. PointsPlus method.

Original Points (before November 2010)

Estimated original Points = max(0, round(kcal / 50 + total fat (g) / 12 − min(fiber (g), 4) / 5)). The fiber deduction is limited to 4 grams. Calories here mean food kilocalories (kcal), not joules.

Old Daily Target Estimate

The fourth calculator uses a community-reconstructed historical expression: max(18, round((base − 9.2 × age + activity coefficient × weight (kg) + 6.2 × height (cm)) / 213)). Base is 864 for male or 760 for female; activity factors range from 85.2–112.59 (male) or 62.34–89.73 (female). The middle two activity levels are interpolated approximations, not confirmed official coefficients. Daily targets may differ substantially from those once issued by WW.

Worked Examples

For 250 kcal, 10 g sugar, 3 g saturated fat, and 12 g protein, the SmartPoints estimate is 0.0305 × 250 + 0.12 × 10 + 0.275 × 3 − 0.098 × 12 = 8.474, which rounds to 8 points.

For 8 g protein, 31 g carbohydrate, 5 g fat and 3 g fiber, PointsPlus = (128 + 589 + 225 − 42) / 175 = 5.14, or 5 points. For 200 kcal, 5 g fat and 10 g fiber, original Points = 200 / 50 + 5 / 12 − 4 / 5 = 3.62, or 4 points.

Units and Results

Energy can be entered as food calories (kcal), kilojoules or joules. Nutrients can be entered as grams, kilograms, ounces or pounds. All food calculations convert measurements to kcal and grams before applying their formula. Points are rounded to whole numbers, and food point results cannot be negative. A point value computed from nutrients may not match a food's official zero-point designation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the latest calculator the current WW program?

No. The reference page calls its 2015–2021 SmartPoints calculator “Latest,” but current WW Points rules have changed. The calculation uses an independent approximation and is not an official WW points lookup.

Does the daily target show my recommended calorie intake?

No. It is an estimate of a historical points budget, not a dietary prescription, calorie goal, or personalized medical assessment.

Can I enter a whole recipe?

Yes, if you enter the recipe's combined nutrition totals, the calculator estimates points for the whole recipe. To get points per portion, divide each nutrient amount by the number of servings before calculating; rounding the completed recipe's point total can give a different answer.

Why do official WW values sometimes differ?

The official program can include food classifications, zero-point foods, individual plans, specialized ingredient treatment and updated proprietary equations. The estimates on this page intentionally do not claim to reproduce those decisions.
