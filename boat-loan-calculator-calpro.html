    How to Use the Boat Loan Calculator

    Use this calculator to estimate a fixed-rate boat loan's monthly payment, cash needed at purchase, total interest, and overall boat purchase cost. Choose Total Price to start with a boat's price, or Monthly Payment to estimate the boat price supported by a desired payment.

    1. Enter the boat price (or target monthly payment), repayment term, and annual interest rate.
    2. Enter a dollar or percentage down payment, any trade-in value, the expected sales tax rate, and purchasing fees.
    3. Choose whether to include sales tax and all listed fees in the loan, then press Calculate.
    4. Review the payment, purchase cost and principal-versus-interest chart, then switch between annual and monthly amortization schedules. The Export CSV option saves the monthly schedule on demand.

    Boat Loan Formula

    For a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan, the monthly payment is:

    M = P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)−n], where P is the financed principal, r is the annual interest rate divided by 12 and 100, and n is the number of monthly payments. For a 0% interest rate, M = P ÷ n.

    With taxes and fees paid upfront, P = boat price − down payment − trade-in value. If you check Include all fees in loan, the calculator adds sales tax + entered fees to the financed principal. The modeled sales tax is the boat price multiplied by your entered tax rate, without jurisdiction-specific deductions or exemptions.

    Worked Example

    Suppose you buy a $35,000 boat, put $7,000 down, trade in nothing, borrow for 10 years at 7% interest, pay 7% sales tax, and have $2,000 in fees. If tax and fees are paid upfront, the financed balance is $28,000. The estimated payment is $325.10 per month, with $11,012.45 in interest over 120 payments.

    Understanding the Results

    Loan amount is the principal borrowed. Upfront payment is the cash down payment plus any sales tax and fees that are not financed; the trade-in is applied as a separate credit against the loan principal. Total loan interest is the combined loan payments less the original principal. Total cost adds boat price, sales tax, fees, and loan interest; it is not your remaining cash obligation after accounting for trade-in equity.

    In the Monthly Payment tab, the calculator reverses the same payment formula to estimate the boat price based on the selected down payment, trade-in, tax treatment, and fees. This is a budgeting estimate, not a lender approval decision.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does financing sales tax and fees increase the monthly payment?

    Yes. When sales tax and fees are added to principal, the initial cash due at purchase is lower, but you pay interest on those charges for the life of the loan.

    Can I use a down payment percentage instead of dollars?

    Yes. Select % next to Down Payment. The calculator calculates that percentage of the boat's price, including the estimated price in reverse mode.

    What expenses are not included?

    Insurance, marina slips, storage, maintenance, fuel, winterization, repairs, and future registration renewals are excluded unless you explicitly enter a purchase cost in Fees. Actual tax rules, loan charges, early payoff conditions, and monthly bills may differ.

    Are saved estimates private?

    The Save control stores up to 20 estimates locally in your browser, without transmitting them to the reference website. Clearing your browser's site data can remove them.
