How to Use the Time Card Calculator

Enter the start time, finish time, and unpaid break for each day you worked. The first row starts with an example shift of 8:30AM to 5:00PM with a 30-minute break; you can change it before calculating. Leave unused days empty, or select Show blank days in report to include them in your printable summary.

  1. Enter Monday–Sunday shift details. You can rename the days to calendar dates or custom labels.
  2. For time entries, use 8:30AM, 5:00PM, 15:30, 8.30, or 830. For breaks, enter 0:30 or 30 for 30 minutes.
  3. Choose whether to round each clock-in and clock-out to the nearest five, ten, fifteen, thirty, or sixty minutes.
  4. Optionally supply a report header and notes, and enable pay calculations with an hourly rate and your own overtime policy.
  5. Click Calculate. The report appears above the input form; print it or select save to export a CSV file.

Time Card Formula

Daily paid working time is clock-out − clock-in − unpaid breaks, with a next-day clock-out supported for overnight shifts. For example, 8:30AM to 5:00PM is 8 hours 30 minutes; subtracting a 30-minute break gives 8 hours. Sum the daily values to obtain the weekly total.

When a rounding option is selected, this calculator independently rounds each clock-in and clock-out to the nearest selected increment before subtracting the exact break. With No Round, the entered minutes are used unchanged.

Hourly Pay and Overtime

Regular earnings = regular hours × base hourly rate. When overtime pay is enabled, overtime earnings = overtime hours × base hourly rate × overtime multiplier. Total gross pay equals regular earnings plus overtime earnings. For a weekly overtime threshold of 40 hours, 45 hours at $20/hour and a 1.5× overtime rate yields 40 × $20 + 5 × $20 × 1.5 = $950.

The daily and weekly overtime methods are separate selectable rules, not additive. The “pay maximum” options cap compensated hours without erasing actual worked hours from the report. All currency amounts are estimates before tax and other deductions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the calculator support overnight shifts?

Yes. For an explicit end time earlier than the start time, the calculator treats the finish as occurring on the following day. Use AM/PM or 24-hour notation whenever a shift may be ambiguous.

How should I enter lunch breaks?

Use 0:30, 0.30, or 30 to deduct 30 minutes. A time with a colon or decimal point uses hours and minutes, while a whole number is treated as a number of minutes.

Can I save or print the weekly report?

Yes. Select Save values to this browser to retain your form entries locally on this device. The report's save control exports a CSV file. Use Print to print the report or choose “Save as PDF” from your browser's print dialog. The tool does not send your entries to a server.

Does it automatically apply labor law overtime rules?

No. Overtime thresholds and payment multipliers vary by location, work agreement, and employee classification. Choose the appropriate policy yourself and confirm the resulting payroll figures against applicable requirements. For U.S. recordkeeping and time rounding information, refer to the U.S. Department of Labor recordkeeping guidance and hours-worked and rounding guidance.
