How to Use the Percent Off Calculator

Enter any two of these values: original price, percent off, final price, or amount saved. Leave the other two fields blank, then select Calculate. For example, you can enter the original price and percentage to get the sale price, or enter both prices to find the percentage discount.

To apply a coupon after the initial discount, choose Yes under “Has stackable additional discount?” and enter the extra percentage. This second discount is applied to the already-reduced price. Select Clear to enter a new pair of values. Select the save icon in the results heading to download a text summary.

Percent Off Formulas

With original price P, first discount rate d₁, and optional second discount d₂ expressed as percentages:

  • Final price = P × (1 − d₁/100) × (1 − d₂/100).
  • Amount saved = Original price − Final price.
  • Discount percentage (without a second discount) = (Amount saved / Original price) × 100, for a nonzero original price.
  • Overall percentage saved = [1 − (1 − d₁/100) × (1 − d₂/100)] × 100.

The calculator displays money to two decimal places. For reverse calculations, it derives the missing value from the chosen two inputs; the inferred percentage can vary slightly due to cent rounding.

Worked Examples

Single 15% Discount

A $39.99 item with 15% off has a calculated final price of $39.99 × 0.85 = $33.99 after rounding. The savings are $6.00.

Two Stackable Discounts

Suppose an item costs $279.00 and gets 20% off, followed by another 15% off. The first discount reduces it to $223.20. The second saves another $33.48, producing a $189.72 final price and $89.28 total savings. Together, the discounts equal an effective 32% reduction, not 35%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate the percentage off if I know the original and sale prices?

Yes. Fill in the original and final price fields and leave the percentage and savings fields empty. The calculator computes the missing percentage and displays the savings.

Can I enter the amount saved instead of the final price?

Yes. Enter the original price and saved amount to derive the final price and discount rate. You can also combine final price and savings to find the original price and percentage discount.

Are 20% off and an additional 15% off the same as 35% off?

No. Successive discounts multiply their remaining-price factors. The total reduction in this example is 32%.

What happens if the discount is 100%?

An original price and a 100% discount determine a $0 final price. However, a $0 final price alone cannot reveal the original price when the discount is 100%. The calculator flags pairs that do not have a unique solution.

Are taxes and shipping included?

No. The calculator handles percentage discounts only. Apply any sales taxes, shipping charges, or fees separately using the store's rules.
