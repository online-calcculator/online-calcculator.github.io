How to Use the Mulch Calculator

Choose Use Total Area if you already know the size of the space. Enter the total and select square feet, square meters, square yards, or acres. Alternatively, select Use Dimensions and enter the length and width of each rectangular section; you can calculate up to 12 separate areas using different units.

Enter the desired mulch layer depth and its unit. If you know the mulch price, enter the price and select its volume or bag-size basis. Click Calculate to see the mulch volume and estimated total cost. Clear restores the starting example. The result's save control opens the browser's Print dialog, where you can print or save as PDF.

Mulch Volume Formula

The basic formula is mulch volume = coverage area × mulch layer depth. Use consistent units before multiplying. When you enter multiple rectangular sections, the calculator adds their individual areas (length × width) before applying the mulch depth.

Worked Example: 200 Square Meters at 2 cm

A garden of 200 m² with a 2 cm (0.02 m) mulch layer needs 200 × 0.02 = 4 m³ of mulch, equal to 4,000 liters, approximately 5.23 cubic yards, or 141.3 cubic feet. At $20 per cubic meter, the estimated material cost is $80.00, excluding any extra delivery charges and waste allowance.

How Deep Should Mulch Be?

Appropriate depth depends on the mulch and planting conditions. As general guidance, a 2–4 inch (about 5–10 cm) layer is often used, but overly thick mulch can restrict water or air. Check guidance for your particular material and avoid piling it against trunks or plant stems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate multiple garden beds at once?

Yes. In dimensions mode, enter up to three rectangular sections immediately, or choose “Add more areas” to enter as many as twelve. Leave unused rows blank. Each row can use its own unit.

What if my yard is an irregular shape?

Break the landscape into approximate rectangles and add their areas using dimensions mode. For curved edges, approximate the total square footage separately and use total-area mode.

Does the price estimate include delivery and taxes?

No. The estimated cost reflects only the price per chosen volume or bag size. Delivery, taxes, coverage loss, and possible extra material are not included.

Does the calculator estimate how many bags to purchase?

It calculates total required volume and can estimate cost from a selected bag volume. To find the number of whole bags, divide the required mulch volume by the bag size and round up. Consider a modest additional allowance if your project requires it.
