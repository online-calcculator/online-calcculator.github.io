How to Use the Time Zone Calculator

Choose the source date and enter the local time using 24-hour notation, such as 15:03:09 for 3:03:09 PM. Select a fixed From UTC offset and a To UTC offset, then click Calculate. The source and converted time—including the weekday—will appear above the form. Use the calendar button to choose a date or the small Save control in the result bar to save a text copy.

Time Zone Conversion Formula

The calculator converts the entered wall-clock time into UTC and then applies the destination offset:

Destination time = source time + (destination UTC offset − source UTC offset).

Offsets are in hours and minutes. For example, converting November 5, 2026 at 15:03:09 from UTC+05:00 to UTC−05:00 subtracts 10 hours, producing November 5, 2026 at 05:03:09. When the difference crosses midnight, the calculator adjusts the calendar date and weekday. A difference as large as 26 hours is possible when converting between UTC−12:00 and UTC+14:00.

Understanding Fixed UTC Offsets

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) is the shared reference for time offsets. UTC+05:45 is 5 hours and 45 minutes ahead of UTC; UTC−03:30 is 3 hours and 30 minutes behind it. This calculator uses the numerical offsets chosen in the dropdowns, not the time-zone rules of any particular city.

Daylight saving time: This calculator does not automatically apply seasonal clock changes. If a region observes daylight saving time, select the offset actually in effect for the date being converted. Region names in the dropdowns are examples, not a live location-based lookup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert to another day or year?

Yes. The calculation carries the full date across midnight, month endings, leap days, and year boundaries.

Can it handle half-hour and quarter-hour time zones?

Yes. It includes offsets such as UTC+03:30, UTC+05:30, UTC+05:45, UTC+08:45, UTC+09:30, and UTC+12:45.

Does the calculator know if New York, London, or Sydney is on daylight saving time?

No. Its inputs are fixed offsets. For actual historical or future local civil time, use the appropriate date-specific offset based on a trusted zone database.

What input time format should I use?

Use 24-hour time: HH:mm or HH:mm:ss. Examples: 00:00, 09:05, and 23:59:59. Seconds are interpreted as zero if omitted.
