Love Calculator

The Love Calculator provides a playful score based on the names of two people. The higher the percentage, the higher the name-match score in this entertainment-only game.

Note that, like other love calculators, this tool is intended for amusement only. It does not provide a real indication of love; make relationship decisions based on your experiences, communication, and judgment rather than a numerical score.

How to Use the Love Calculator

Enter the name of each person and select Calculate. A playful score and short explanation appear directly above the name fields. You can change either name and calculate again, use Clear to start over, or select Save to download the displayed result as a PNG image.

You can use first names, full names, or nicknames. Keep the spelling consistent when comparing results, since changing the letters changes the score. No birthdays, gender selections, or extra details are required.

How Is the Love Score Calculated?

This is a name-based arithmetic game, not a scientific relationship assessment. CalcPro trims surrounding whitespace and converts both names to lowercase. It then adds the numerical UTF-16 code-unit values for each character in each name, multiplies those two totals, and uses the remainder after dividing by 100 as the score:

Entertainment formula: Score = (sum of character codes in name 1 × sum of character codes in name 2) mod 100.

Because the formula uses a remainder of 100, its possible outputs are whole numbers from 0% through 99%, even though the display is conventionally described as a percentage out of 100. It does not represent a measured chance of a successful relationship.

Worked Example: Dog and Duck

For Dog, the lowercase letters have character codes 100, 111, and 103, totaling 314. For Duck, the lowercase character codes 100, 117, 99, and 107 total 423. Multiplying gives 314 × 423 = 132,822. The remainder after dividing by 100 is 22, so the displayed score is 22%. This matches the example on the reference page.

Understanding Your Result

The score is accompanied by a lighthearted message. Larger numbers receive a more enthusiastic message, but those messages are part of the game and are not relationship guidance. The calculation does not evaluate trust, respect, communication, shared goals, affection, or how either person behaves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Love Calculator accurate?

No. The output depends only on the characters in two names. It is intended for amusement, not as evidence about romantic compatibility or future outcomes.

Does switching the two names change the result?

No. Multiplication gives the same number regardless of which name is entered first, so reversing the pair does not alter the score.

Will I get the same result again?

Yes. The algorithm is deterministic: the same two names, with the same spelling, produce the same score. Capitalization and surrounding whitespace do not affect the result.

Why can changing one letter make a difference?

Each character contributes a numerical value to its name total. Changing the characters changes the multiplication and may change the final remainder.

Are the names sent to a server?

No. This CalcPro version performs the calculation inside your browser. It does not send the entered names to a calculator API or store them in browser storage. When you select Save, the image is generated locally in your browser.

Why does the result never show 100%?

A remainder after division by 100 can be as low as 0 or as high as 99, but cannot equal 100.
