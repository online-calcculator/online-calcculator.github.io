How the Quick Estimates Work

Fixed-Rate Mortgage

For loan principal P, monthly interest rate r (annual percentage rate divided by 1,200) and n monthly payments, the principal-and-interest payment is M = P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)−n]. For a zero-interest loan, M = P ÷ n. Property taxes, insurance, HOA dues and any user-estimated PMI are added separately. The annual amortization schedule calculates interest on the balance remaining each month.

Home Affordability

The estimator takes the lower of a housing-to-income budget and a total-debt-to-income budget after existing debt payments. It deducts estimated taxes, insurance and HOA charges, calculates the affordable loan principal using the mortgage payment factor, and adjusts for the down-payment percentage. The default percentage limits are adjustable examples, not lender approval criteria.

Down Payment and Cash to Close

The down payment is the purchase price multiplied by the down-payment percentage. Estimated initial cash equals the down payment plus the entered closing-cost estimate; cash still needed is reduced by any earnest-money deposit already paid. Actual settlement statements may include prepaid expenses and credits.

Rental Property Performance

Estimated net operating income (NOI) equals collected rent minus recurring operating expenses, property taxes and insurance, before mortgage debt service. Capitalization rate equals annual NOI divided by purchase price. Monthly cash flow is NOI per month minus the mortgage principal-and-interest payment. Cash-on-cash return is annual cash flow divided by estimated initial cash invested. A negative return signals a modeled cash shortfall.

Rent Budget and Property Area

The rent budget compares a target percentage of monthly take-home income with the cash available after debts, essentials and planned savings, using the lower value. The area calculator supports a rectangle (length × width), triangle (base × perpendicular height ÷ 2) and circle (π × radius²). Conversions use 1 foot = 0.3048 meters and 1 acre = 43,560 square feet.

Worked Examples

For a $400,000 home with 20% down, the loan principal is $320,000. At a fixed 6.5% annual rate for 30 years, principal and interest are approximately $2,022.62 per month, before property tax, insurance, and fees. A 40-by-30-foot rectangle measures 1,200 ft², or approximately 111.48 m².

Frequently Asked Questions

Are taxes and insurance included in the mortgage payment?

They are included in the tool's estimated all-in monthly cost if you enter them, but not in its principal-and-interest or total-loan-interest calculations. The actual lender payment may differ.

Does a higher capitalization rate mean guaranteed profit?

No. Cap rate describes annual property operating income relative to price. It ignores financing costs, income taxes, appreciation, sale costs and many potential risks.

Are these affordability estimates loan approvals?

No. Approval depends on lender requirements, location, credit history, verified expenses and other underwriting considerations. The editable ratios are budgeting assumptions.

Can I use a currency other than U.S. dollars?

Yes. Choose USD, PKR, GBP, EUR, INR, CAD or AUD. The selector changes only the denomination displayed on monetary results; it does not convert existing entered amounts at exchange rates.
