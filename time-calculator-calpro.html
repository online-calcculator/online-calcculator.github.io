How to Use the Time Calculator

CalcPro's time calculator offers three ways to work with durations. To add or subtract two durations, enter days, hours, minutes and seconds in the first two rows, select Add or Subtract, and choose Calculate. Empty fields count as zero, and the final row shows normalized days, hours, minutes and seconds.

To move forward or backward from a particular date, choose the start date and clock time under Add or Subtract Time from a Date. Enter the duration and calculate the resulting calendar date, clock time and day of the week. Use Now to populate the current time from your device. To combine more than two durations, type an expression such as 2h 15m + 45m - 30s.

Time Addition and Subtraction Formula

Convert every duration into seconds before performing arithmetic. The conversions are exact for fixed-length durations: 1 day = 86,400 seconds, 1 hour = 3,600 seconds, and 1 minute = 60 seconds.

Total seconds = days × 86,400 + hours × 3,600 + minutes × 60 + seconds.

For addition, add the two totals. For subtraction, subtract the second total from the first. Then divide and take remainders successively by 86,400, 3,600 and 60 to display normalized days, hours, minutes and seconds.

Worked Example: Adding Time

Suppose your first duration is 2 hours and 45 minutes, and your second duration is 1 hour and 30 minutes. Their total is 9,900 + 5,400 = 15,300 seconds, which is 4 hours and 15 minutes.

Worked Example: Adding Time to a Date

Start on February 28, 2024, at 11:30 PM. Adding 2 hours produces February 29, 2024, at 1:30 AM. February has 29 days in 2024 because it is a leap year. Subtracting a duration uses the same fixed-second arithmetic in the opposite direction.

Understanding Your Results

The result panel appears immediately above the calculator you use, as on the reference page. A duration result provides a normalized breakdown as well as equivalent total days, weeks, hours, minutes and seconds. A negative duration means the amount subtracted was larger than the starting duration.

The date calculator interprets a day as exactly 24 hours and uses Gregorian calendar rollover across months and leap years. It does not apply any time zone or daylight-saving adjustment. For example, shifting by 24 hours is treated as one complete 24-hour interval, even on dates when a local civil day may be shorter or longer. The results are rounded to the nearest millisecond when decimal values are entered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I leave fields empty?

Yes. Empty duration fields are zero. Date and clock inputs must contain valid values. You can enter values greater than 59 minutes or seconds in duration fields; CalcPro automatically carries them into larger units.

Can I subtract more time than I started with?

Yes. The calculator displays a negative result with the absolute hours, minutes and seconds. For instance, 1 hour minus 2 hours and 30 minutes gives −1 hour and 30 minutes.

What does the expression calculator support?

Write values followed by d, h, m or s, separated by + and -. For example, 1d 3h + 25m - 2h is valid. Multiplication, division, unitless numbers and parentheses are not supported.

Is this suitable for time zones and daylight-saving time?

Use it for elapsed durations and simple date arithmetic, not for converting between time zones or scheduling across daylight-saving transitions. Those applications require a time-zone-aware calendar.

How large may the durations be?

The duration calculator accepts totals of up to one trillion seconds. The date calculator supports resulting calendar years 1 through 9999. Inputs are checked and an inline message appears if a calculation exceeds these ranges.
