How to Use the Savings Calculator

Enter a starting balance, planned annual and monthly deposits, and any annual increase to those deposits. Choose the nominal interest rate, compounding frequency, number of years, and tax rate on interest. Click Calculate to estimate your ending savings balance, then switch between the annual and monthly accumulation schedules.

Savings Calculation Formula

The calculator derives an effective monthly interest rate from the stated nominal annual interest rate and compounding frequency:

Monthly effective rate: i = (1 + r / n)^(n / 12) - 1, where r is the annual rate expressed as a decimal and n is the number of compounding periods per year. For continuous compounding, i = e^(r / 12) - 1.

Each month, the balance earns interest based on its opening balance. Any tax on interest is incorporated in the effective rate for the selected compounding period. The monthly contribution is then added, followed by the annual contribution if it is the twelfth month of a savings year. Contribution increases are applied at the start of each new savings year.

Worked Savings Example

With a $20,000 initial deposit, $5,000 contributed at the end of each year, 3% growth in that annual contribution, no monthly contributions, a 3% annual rate compounded annually, no tax, and a 10-year horizon, the estimated ending balance is $92,116.99. This consists of $20,000 in the original deposit, $57,319.40 in additional contributions, and approximately $14,797.59 in earned interest.

Understanding the Results

The results table separates your starting principal from later contributions and interest. The doughnut chart illustrates those amounts when all three are nonnegative, while the accumulation chart and schedule show how savings build over time. Negative contributions can model withdrawals and may produce a negative balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are contributions deposited?

Monthly contributions are deposited at each month-end. Annual contributions occur at each year-end, so they do not earn interest during the year in which they are deposited.

What does the tax rate apply to?

The tax percentage applies only to the interest earned, not to the original deposit or your contributions. Enter zero if your interest income is tax-exempt or you wish to ignore tax.

Are inflation and bank fees included?

No. The output is a nominal dollar estimate before bank fees; it does not adjust purchasing power for inflation. The reference calculator mentions inflation in its general description but does not offer an inflation-rate field. For an inflation-adjusted estimate, divide the projected balance by (1 + inflation rate)^years.

How does compounding frequency affect savings?

For a positive nominal interest rate, more frequent compounding usually produces a higher effective annual yield. The calculator uses a monthly-equivalent rate so that monthly and yearly cash flows can be compared consistently.
