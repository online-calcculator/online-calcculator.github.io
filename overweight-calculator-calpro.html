How to Use the Overweight Calculator

Enter your age, gender, height, and weight, then choose Calculate. The result identifies your adult body mass index (BMI) category and shows the approximate normal-weight range for your height. The result appears immediately to the right of the fields on larger screens and beneath them on smaller screens.

Select Metric Units to enter centimeters and kilograms; US Units to enter feet, inches, and pounds; or Other Units to choose meters, inches, feet, yards, kilograms, pounds, stone, or ounces. Measurements are converted when you switch units. Use Clear to restore sample values, or select save to download a text record of the current result.

Overweight Calculator Formula

This tool uses the standard adult BMI equation, with measured weight in kilograms and height in meters:

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ [height (m)]²

To calculate the adult normal-weight range for a particular height, multiply the square of the height in meters by the BMI boundaries of 18.5 and 25:

Lower weight = 18.5 × height²
Upper weight threshold = 25 × height²

The upper figure is a boundary, not an inclusive normal-weight limit: the normal BMI category is 18.5 up to, but not including, 25. The displayed weights are rounded to one decimal place.

Worked Example: 180 cm and 65 kg

Convert 180 cm to 1.80 m. The BMI is 65 ÷ (1.80 × 1.80) = 20.1 to one decimal place, which is in the normal adult range. The lower weight is 18.5 × 1.80² = 59.94 kg, and the upper threshold is 25 × 1.80² = 81.00 kg. Therefore, the calculator displays 59.9–81.0 kg.

Understanding Your Weight Status

Adult BMIWeight status
Below 18.5Underweight
18.5 to under 25.0Normal (healthy weight)
25.0 to under 30.0Overweight
30.0 or aboveObesity

BMI is a screening indicator, not a direct measurement of body fat and not a medical diagnosis. Athletes, people with greater muscle mass, and others can have a BMI that does not fully reflect their health. Discuss health concerns and individual risk with a qualified healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does age or gender change the adult BMI formula?

No. For adults aged 20 years and older, the standard BMI calculation and its category thresholds do not differ by age or gender. The inputs are retained for completeness and saved results, but do not change the numeric BMI calculation.

Can this calculator assess overweight status for someone under 20?

No. Children and teens aged 2–19 are assessed using sex- and age-specific BMI-for-age percentiles rather than the adult cutoffs. For that reason, this calculator requests an adult age of 20–120.

Why do unit conversions sometimes change the last decimal?

Feet, inches, pounds, stone, and metric units are converted using standard conversion factors. Input rounding after a unit switch can produce a tiny difference in the last displayed decimal.

Is the displayed normal-weight range a personalized target?

No. It is a mathematical range derived from adult BMI thresholds. It does not account for medical history, body composition, pregnancy, or other clinical considerations.
