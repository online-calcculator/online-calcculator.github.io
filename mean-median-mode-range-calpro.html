Mean, Median, Mode, Range and Geometric Mean Formulas

Arithmetic Mean (Average)

The arithmetic mean is the sum of the observations divided by their count: Mean = (x₁ + x₂ + … + xₙ) / n. The result uses every value, so unusually large or small observations can affect it.

Median

Sort the observations from smallest to largest. With an odd count, select the middle number. With an even count, average the two central numbers: Median = (middle-left + middle-right) / 2.

Mode

The mode is the value occurring most often. The calculator reports all tied modes, preserving the order in which those values first appeared. If every entry occurs once, it reports no mode.

Range

Range = maximum − minimum. Range indicates the spread between the two extreme observations; it does not tell you how data are distributed between them.

Geometric Mean

For positive observations, Geometric mean = (x₁ × x₂ × … × xₙ)1/n. To avoid overflowing with large products, the calculator evaluates the equivalent logarithmic formula, exp((ln x₁ + … + ln xₙ)/n). A zero with otherwise nonnegative values gives a geometric mean of zero. For data containing negative values, the calculator reports the geometric mean as undefined under the nonnegative-real convention used here.

Worked Example

For 10, 2, 38, 23, 38, 23, 21, the sorted set is 2, 10, 21, 23, 23, 38, 38. The sum is 155 and there are 7 observations, so the mean is 155 ÷ 7 ≈ 22.142857142857. The median is 23. Both 38 and 23 occur twice and therefore share the mode. The range is 38 − 2 = 36, and the geometric mean is approximately 16.412764443111.

Understanding the Results

The mean is useful for many numerical data sets, while the median can be more representative when extreme values are present. The mode highlights frequently recurring observations, and the range describes the full span. The chart shows each original observation, not its frequency, so repeated inputs each have their own column.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a data set have multiple modes?

Yes. Two or more values can tie for the highest frequency; all tied values are shown. A list with no repeated values is labeled as having no mode.

How does the median work for an even count?

The calculator sorts the numbers and takes the arithmetic average of the two values in the middle. For 1, 3, 8, 10, the median is (3 + 8) / 2 = 5.5.

Does the calculator accept decimals and negative values?

Yes. Arithmetic mean, median, range, modes, sum and chart support decimals and negative numbers. The geometric mean is not reported as a real-valued number for sets containing negatives.

What if every number is identical?

The minimum, maximum, mean, median and mode all equal that number, the range is zero, and the count is the number of entries.
