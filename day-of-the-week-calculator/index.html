How to Use the Day of the Week Calculator

Select a month and day, enter a year from 1 through 9999, and click Calculate. You can also use the calendar icon to choose a date visually. The result appears above the date form without navigating to another page. Click a date in the displayed month calendar to recalculate for that day, use Save to download a text report, or choose Reset to return to today's date.

What Does the Calculator Show?

For the selected date, the calculator reports its weekday (Monday through Sunday), its ordinal position within the year, the number of days remaining in the year, and the selected weekday's occurrence number and total count during that calendar year. A Sunday-first calendar of the selected month highlights the chosen date.

How the Weekday Is Calculated

The calculator uses the Gregorian calendar and exact integer day counts rather than milliseconds. A year is a leap year when it is divisible by 4, except for years divisible by 100 unless they are also divisible by 400. Thus 2000 and 2024 are leap years, while 1900 and 2100 are not.

Let Y be the selected year, and let N be the day number within that year. First count the days before January 1 of year Y:

Days before year Y = 365 × (Y − 1) + ⌊(Y − 1) / 4⌋ − ⌊(Y − 1) / 100⌋ + ⌊(Y − 1) / 400⌋.
Weekday index = (days before year Y + N) mod 7, where 0 = Sunday, 1 = Monday, …, 6 = Saturday.

For a particular weekday, locate its first occurrence in January, then count forward in seven-day increments. This yields the weekday's occurrence number and the total number of times that weekday appears in the year.

Worked Example: November 4, 2026

November 4, 2026 falls on a Wednesday. It is day 308 of a 365-day year, leaving 57 days after that date. It is the 44th Wednesday of the year; 2026 contains 52 Wednesdays, so 8 Wednesdays remain afterward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I find the weekday for a historical or future date?

Yes. Enter any year from 1 through 9999. For historical dates, the calculator applies the proleptic Gregorian calendar (extending today's Gregorian rules backward). Historical countries adopted the Gregorian calendar at different times, so results may differ from dates recorded under older calendar systems.

Are leap years handled automatically?

Yes. February has 29 days in a Gregorian leap year and 28 otherwise. The day dropdown adjusts when you change the month or year, and invalid years are rejected.

Does the calendar start on Sunday or Monday?

The visual calendar starts on Sunday to match the reference layout. ISO 8601 uses Monday as the first weekday, but that does not change the weekday of any particular date.

Does the calculation depend on my time zone?

No. Once you choose a date, the result is based only on its calendar year, month, and day. Your device's local date is used solely to prefill the form and for the Reset and Today controls.
