How to Use the Body Type Calculator

Enter four circumference measurements: bust (the fullest part of your chest), waist (your natural narrowest waist), high hip (around the upper hip/pelvis), and hip (the widest part of your hips and buttocks). Keep the measuring tape level, comfortable, and snug without compressing your body. Choose centimeters or inches separately for each input, then click Calculate.

The body-shape label and waist-to-hip ratio will appear above the fields. Click Clear to empty them, or use Save in the results bar to open your browser’s print dialog, where you can print the page or save it as a PDF.

Body Shape Formulas and Classifications

This calculator uses the seven commonly published Female Figure Identification Technique (FFIT) shape rules. All circumference differences are converted to inches before testing the thresholds, even when the original measurements are in centimeters. Shapes are evaluated in order; if none matches, the calculator reports “Not classified” rather than assigning a misleading label.

ShapePrimary rule (measurements in inches)
HourglassB − H ≤ 1, H − B < 3.6, and either B − W ≥ 9 or H − W ≥ 10
Bottom hourglass3.6 ≤ H − B < 10, H − W ≥ 9, and HH ÷ W < 1.193
Top hourglass1 < B − H < 10, and B − W ≥ 9
SpoonH − B > 2, H − W ≥ 7, and HH ÷ W ≥ 1.193
TriangleH − B ≥ 3.6 and H − W < 9
Inverted triangleB − H ≥ 3.6 and B − W < 9
RectangleH − B < 3.6, B − H < 3.6, B − W < 9, and H − W < 10

Notation: B = bust; W = waist; HH = high hip; H = hip. One inch equals exactly 2.54 cm.

Waist-to-Hip Ratio Formula

WHR = Waist circumference ÷ Hip circumference. Both must be in the same unit. For example, a 60 cm waist and a 90 cm hip give 60 ÷ 90 = 0.6667, shown as 0.67.

Example: Bust 90 cm, waist 60 cm, high hip 80 cm, and hip 90 cm → Hourglass; waist-to-hip ratio 0.67.

What Do the Results Mean?

Body-shape categories describe clothing-fit proportions and are not a medical diagnosis, ideal, or judgment of health. People have diverse and changing proportions, and a small measurement difference can change the category. Waist-to-hip ratio has been studied as one marker of abdominal fat distribution, but its interpretation depends on other factors and cannot alone determine individual health risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I mix centimeters and inches?

Yes. Each field has its own unit selector. The calculator converts every measurement to inches before applying the classification rules. Changing the unit automatically converts the displayed number to preserve the measured size.

Where do I measure my high hip?

Measure around the upper hip/pelvic area, commonly about 7 inches (18 cm) below your natural waist. Do not confuse this with your full hip measurement, taken at the widest point.

Why does my result say “Not classified”?

The published thresholds do not cover every possible set of proportions. Rather than guess, the calculator explicitly states when none of the seven categories applies. Your WHR is still calculated.

Is an hourglass shape healthier than another shape?

No. The shape labels are for apparel fit and styling, not a health ranking. Discuss medical questions about waist measurements or cardiometabolic risk with a qualified clinician.
