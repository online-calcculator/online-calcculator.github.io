How to Use the Age Calculator

Choose your birth month, day, and year, then enter the date on which you want to know your age. Select Calculate to see completed years and months plus the remaining days. The result also shows the same time span as total months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

Both dates default to today's date according to your device. You can change either date using the dropdowns or the calendar icons. Use Reset dates to restore today's dates, or save in the result bar to download a text summary.

Age Calculation Formula

Chronological age is the number of complete calendar years, then complete months, then days between a date of birth and a comparison date. The calculator counts full calendar months without assuming that every month has 30 days:

  1. Find the greatest number of complete calendar months between the two selected dates.
  2. Divide completed months into years (12 months = 1 year) and leftover months.
  3. Count the remaining full calendar days after the last completed month.
  4. Calculate total days as the exact difference between the dates; convert those days into weeks, hours, minutes, and seconds.
Elapsed time units: 1 week = 7 calendar days; 1 day = 24 hours; 1 hour = 60 minutes; 1 minute = 60 seconds. Hours, minutes, and seconds are date-based equivalents because no birth time or time zone is entered.

Worked Examples

Age on a particular date

Someone born on June 15, 2000, is 26 years, 4 months, and 20 days old on November 4, 2026. The last completed month falls on October 15, and the remaining interval is 20 days.

Uneven month lengths

February 28, 2022, to March 31, 2022, is shown as 1 month and 3 days: February 28 to March 28 is one completed month, followed by three more days. The same period contains 31 calendar days.

What the Results Mean

The primary result gives the completed calendar years, months, and leftover days. The alternative lines convert the interval to total months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, and seconds. The small calendar shows the comparison month, highlighting the selected date in yellow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate age on an earlier or future date?

Yes. Change “Age at the Date of” to any date on or after the birth date. This is useful for determining age at a past milestone or a future event.

How are leap years and February 29 handled?

The calculator uses actual Gregorian month lengths. If a calendar-month anniversary does not exist, it uses that month's last day; for example, a February 29 birthday reaches its annual anniversary on February 28 in a non-leap year for this calculator's calendar-age convention. Legal birthday conventions can differ by jurisdiction.

Why is my result different from years multiplied by 365?

Years contain 365 or 366 days, while months have different lengths. Dividing a date interval by an average year or month can produce a different result from calendar-age arithmetic.

Are the hour and second totals measured from my exact birth time?

No. This date-only calculator counts whole days. Multiplying calendar days by 24 hours or 86,400 seconds gives elapsed-day equivalents, not a time-of-birth-adjusted result.

What happens if the comparison date is before the date of birth?

The form asks you to choose a comparison date on or after the birth date and displays a validation message otherwise.
