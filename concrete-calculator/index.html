How to Use the Concrete Calculator

Select the shape that best matches your project: slab, column, hollow cylinder, curb-and-gutter barrier, or solid stairs. Enter each dimension in the unit you measured. You can mix units in the same calculation, such as a 5-foot length and a 5-inch slab thickness. Enter how many identical slabs, columns, tubes, or curb sections you need. For stairs, enter the number of risers (vertical steps) rather than a quantity of staircases.

Click Calculate to see the result immediately above that shape's form; no page reload is necessary. The calculator reports cubic feet, cubic yards, cubic meters, estimated concrete weight, and equivalent numbers of 60-lb or 80-lb bags. Click Clear to return that form to its example inputs. The Save and Print controls allow you to retain your current estimate.

Concrete Volume Formulas

All dimensions are converted to a common unit before applying the formulas. The volume of each shape is then converted to cubic feet, cubic yards, and cubic meters.

ShapeFormulaMeasurement notes
Slab, square footing, or wallV = L × W × T × QLength × width × thickness/height × quantity.
Round column or holeV = π × (D/2)² × H × QDiameter, depth/height, and number of cylinders.
Circular slab or hollow tubeV = π/4 × (Dₒ² − Dᵢ²) × H × QSubtract the circular inner opening; the inner diameter must be smaller than the outer diameter. Use zero for a solid cylinder.
Curb and gutterV = [C × (H + T) + G × T] × L × QCurb depth C, curb height H above the flag, flag thickness T, gutter width G, and length L.
Solid stairs with platformV = W × R × [D × N × (N − 1)/2 + P × N]Width W, rise R, tread run D, platform depth P, and N risers. Assumes a solid concrete stair section and a landing at full stair height.

Worked Concrete Examples

5 ft × 2.5 ft slab, 5 in thick

Convert thickness: 5 ÷ 12 = 0.4167 ft. Volume = 5 × 2.5 × (5/12) = 5.21 ft³, or 0.19 yd³ (about 0.15 m³). Estimated weight at 133 lb/ft³ is 692.71 lb. This corresponds to 11.55 bags by mass if each bag holds 60 lb; purchase whole bags and confirm the actual yield.

2.5 ft diameter × 6 ft round footing

Volume = π × (2.5/2)² × 6 = 29.45 ft³, approximately 1.09 yd³.

Solid stairs with five risers

With a 12-in run, 6-in rise, 50-in width, and 5-in platform depth, the four treads below the platform and the full-height landing total approximately 25.17 ft³.

How to Interpret Concrete Bag and Weight Estimates

This calculator follows the reference assumptions of 133 lb per cubic foot for the pounds estimate and 2,130 kg per cubic meter for the metric mass estimate. Values may differ slightly because these are two rounded nominal density assumptions. The displayed bag equivalents use the estimated weight divided by the nominal 60-lb or 80-lb bag size; actual pre-mixed bag yields depend on the product and its mix.

Ordering tip: Round bag counts up to whole bags, then consider extra material for spills, uneven excavation, and waste. For ready-mix orders, check your supplier's minimum delivery size, ordering increments, and recommended waste allowance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between cubic feet and cubic yards?

One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet. Divide a volume in cubic feet by 27 to convert it to cubic yards.

Can I mix inches, feet, and metric units?

Yes. Each measurement has its own unit selector. The calculator converts all entered dimensions before calculating volume.

What if I need a solid column instead of a hollow tube?

Use the round column section, or enter an inner diameter of zero in the tube section.

Does the stair calculator include the landing?

Yes. It assumes a solid concrete stair body with one fewer tread runs than risers and includes the full-height top platform based on the platform depth you enter. It does not account for hollow construction, complex supports, or landings of a different shape.

Why might my bag purchase quantity differ from the result?

Bag counts shown here are weight-based equivalents, not certified mixed-volume yields. A manufacturer's bag label is the more reliable basis for buying premixed concrete. Always round purchases up to whole bags.
