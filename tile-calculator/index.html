How to Use the Tile Calculator

Enter the length and width of one tile and select their units. Choose Use Dimensions for a rectangular floor, wall, or roof, or select Use Total Area if you already know the surface area. Then enter the grout gap, which can be negative when tiles overlap.

Enter the number of tiles in each box and a price if known. Select whether your price applies to a tile, a box, or a square unit of coverage. Choose Calculate to display the estimated number of pieces, boxes, area, optional material cost, and a suggested 5–10% purchasing allowance. Use Clear to restore the example inputs.

Tile Calculator Formula

The calculator converts all dimensions to the same unit, then includes grout spacing or overlap in the effective coverage of one tile:

Effective tile area = (tile length + gap) × (tile width + gap)

Estimated tiles = round up (surface area ÷ effective tile area)

Boxes needed = round up (estimated tiles ÷ tiles per box). The extra allowance uses the unrounded tile estimate multiplied by 1.05 or 1.10, then rounds up. Material cost is based on the selected price unit and the baseline quantity or measured area.

Worked Example

For a surface measuring 20 m × 15 m, the area is 300 m². A 12 cm × 12 cm tile with a 0.25 cm grout gap has an effective area of 0.1225 m × 0.1225 m = 0.01500625 m². Dividing 300 m² by 0.01500625 m² gives approximately 19,991.67, so the calculator estimates 19,992 tiles. At 12 tiles per box, the estimate is 1,666 boxes. Adding 5–10% gives about 20,992–21,991 tiles, or 1,750–1,833 boxes.

Grout Gaps and Overlapping Tiles

A positive gap increases the effective coverage assigned to each tile, so fewer tiles may be required. A negative gap represents overlap, reducing effective coverage. The overlap cannot be so large that either effective tile dimension becomes zero or negative.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the estimate include cutting waste?

The basic tile count is an area-based estimate. The calculator separately provides a 5–10% additional purchasing range for cuts, breakage, and spare tiles. Patterns with many edges or complicated cuts may need more.

Can I use different units for tile size and room dimensions?

Yes. Every dimension is converted internally to meters before the tile count is calculated. Total-area mode supports square feet, square meters, square yards, square inches, and square centimeters.

What happens if I leave box size or price blank?

Leaving box size blank hides the box counts. Leaving price blank hides the cost estimate. A price per box requires a box size.

Is the result an exact tile-by-tile layout?

No. This is an area-based material estimate; it does not model grout joints at the outer edges, staggered patterns, obstacles, or the reuse of cut-off pieces. Verify the final layout and purchase quantities with your installer or supplier.
