How to Use the Gravel Calculator

Select the shape of the area you need to cover. For a measured site, enter its total area; for a rectangular space, enter length and width; for a circular space, enter the diameter. You can mix units such as feet, meters, inches, and centimeters.

Enter your planned gravel depth, then select a material density. Choose Use your own density if your supplier gives a verified bulk-density value. Optionally enter the material's price per unit of volume, weight, or bag. Select Calculate to display the volume, estimated weight, and any applicable cost above the form.

The Clear button restores the initial example. After calculating, you can print your estimate or save it as a text file.

Gravel Volume and Weight Formulas

First calculate area in square meters (m²). The volume of a uniform gravel layer is volume (m³) = area (m²) × depth (m). Weight then follows from weight (kg) = volume (m³) × bulk density (kg/m³).

  • Rectangle: area = length × width.
  • Circle: area = π × (diameter ÷ 2)².
  • Given total area: use the entered area directly after converting it to m².
  • By bag: bag count = round up (required quantity ÷ quantity per bag), then cost = number of bags × price per bag.

Worked Example: 200 m² at 3 cm Deep

For a 200 m² area with a gravel depth of 3 cm (0.03 m) and an assumed bulk density of 1,680 kg/m³: volume = 200 × 0.03 = 6 m³ (6,000 liters). The estimated weight is 6 × 1,680 = 10,080 kg, or 10.08 metric tons. The equivalent volume is approximately 7.85 cubic yards or 211.9 cubic feet.

Understanding the Results

Volume is displayed in liters, cubic meters, cubic yards, and cubic feet. Weight is displayed in kilograms, metric tons, pounds, and U.S. short tons. When a price is supplied, the calculator estimates material cost only. For bag pricing, the number of bags is rounded up to a whole bag.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much gravel do I need for my driveway or pathway?

Measure the surface area and choose an appropriate finished layer depth for the application. Multiply the area by the layer depth; use your supplier's bulk density to convert volume to weight. Requirements can rise with compaction, waste, and uneven base levels.

Can I calculate gravel for a circular fire pit or garden bed?

Yes. Choose Circle and enter the diameter, then select your depth. The calculator converts the circle's area into volume and estimated mass.

What is the difference between a metric ton and a short ton?

A metric ton is 1,000 kilograms; a U.S. short ton is 2,000 pounds, or 907.18474 kilograms. They are different purchasing units, so check which one your supplier uses.

Does the quoted price include delivery or installation?

No. The displayed cost is an estimate for gravel material, based on your entered price. It does not include delivery, installation, labor, taxes, or other fees.
