What Is Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR)?

Glomerular filtration rate estimates how efficiently the kidneys filter blood. This GFR calculator uses serum creatinine and other inputs to estimate kidney filtration normalized to a body surface area of 1.73 m². The result is expressed in mL/min/1.73 m². An estimate does not replace direct kidney-function measurement or medical evaluation.

How to Use the GFR Calculator

  1. For an adult aged 18 or older, enter the serum creatinine measured by your laboratory and choose mg/dL or µmol/L.
  2. Enter age and select sex. The Black/Not Black input is provided solely to reproduce the legacy formulas shown on the reference page; it does not affect the optional 2021 race-free estimate.
  3. Click Calculate. Results appear directly above the adult form, including MDRD, 2009 CKD-EPI, and Mayo estimates. Expand the 2021 CKD-EPI section for a newer race-free comparison.
  4. For a child or adolescent, enter serum creatinine and height in centimeters or inches, then calculate the bedside Schwartz estimate. The pediatric result appears above its form.

Changing a unit automatically converts the numerical entry. Clear empties the values and hides previous results. The Print / Save button opens your browser’s print dialog, where “Save as PDF” is available in supported browsers.

GFR Calculation Formulas

Adult: MDRD Four-Variable Equation (legacy)

eGFR = 175 × SCr−1.154 × Age−0.203 × (0.742 if female) × (1.212 if Black)

SCr is serum creatinine in mg/dL. The coefficient for race is retained to reproduce the historical reference formula, not as a recommended current practice.

Adult: 2009 CKD-EPI Creatinine Equation (legacy)

eGFR = 141 × min(SCr/κ, 1)α × max(SCr/κ, 1)−1.209 × 0.993Age × (1.018 if female) × (1.159 if Black)

For females, κ = 0.7 and α = −0.329. For males, κ = 0.9 and α = −0.411. This is the race-adjusted equation used by the reference calculator.

Adult: Mayo Quadratic Equation

eGFR = exp(1.911 + 5.249/SCr − 2.114/SCr² − 0.00686 × Age − 0.205 if female)

When SCr is below 0.8 mg/dL, 0.8 mg/dL is substituted into this equation.

Adult: 2021 CKD-EPI Creatinine Equation (race-free comparison)

eGFR = 142 × min(SCr/κ, 1)α × max(SCr/κ, 1)−1.200 × 0.9938Age × (1.012 if female)

Here κ is 0.7 for females or 0.9 for males; α is −0.241 for females or −0.302 for males. This is the adult race-free option recommended by kidney organizations for creatinine-only estimation. Creatinine–cystatin C estimates may provide additional accuracy when available.

Pediatric: Bedside Schwartz Equation

eGFR = 0.413 × Height (cm) ÷ SCr (mg/dL)

This height-based equation is designed for pediatric use with standardized serum creatinine. Other pediatric equations, including CKiD U25, may be more appropriate in some circumstances.

Worked Examples

Adult example: A 50-year-old male with SCr = 0.9 mg/dL and the historical “Not Black” setting produces about 89.3 (MDRD), 99.2 (2009 CKD-EPI), and 120.3 (Mayo), each in mL/min/1.73 m². The 2021 race-free formula produces a separate estimate.

Child example: Height 110 cm and SCr 0.9 mg/dL gives 0.413 × 110 ÷ 0.9 = 50.5 mL/min/1.73 m².

Understanding eGFR Results

The GFR categories below describe filtration ranges, not an automatic CKD diagnosis. CKD assessment considers findings lasting at least three months, albuminuria (urine ACR), and other evidence of kidney damage. Categories G1 and G2 do not by themselves establish CKD.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is eGFR the same as measured GFR?

No. eGFR is a calculation using biomarkers and clinical variables. Measured GFR uses specialized clearance techniques and can be used when a more definitive value is needed.

Why do the adult formulas give different results?

They were developed using different populations, modelling assumptions, and statistical coefficients. Comparing results from a single consistent equation over time is more meaningful than comparing values from unrelated equations.

Why is the race input still shown?

It is included to match the older reference calculator’s MDRD and 2009 CKD-EPI calculations. Current US kidney guidance recommends race-free CKD-EPI equations for clinical estimates; use the collapsible 2021 result for this comparison.

Can a single GFR value diagnose chronic kidney disease?

No. An eGFR below 60 or other kidney-damage markers generally need to persist for at least three months, and clinicians use urine albumin and the broader clinical context.

What creatinine units are supported?

Both mg/dL and µmol/L. This calculator uses 88.4 µmol/L per 1 mg/dL. Pediatric height supports centimeters and inches (1 inch = 2.54 cm).
