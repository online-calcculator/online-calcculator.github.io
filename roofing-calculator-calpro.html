      How to Use the Roofing Calculator

      Use the Roofing Area Calculator when you know the area of the building's ground-level footprint. Enter the base area, select its units, choose either roof pitch or roof angle, and provide the eaves overhang. Enter an optional unit price to estimate cost. Select Calculate to see the roof surface area and standard material quantities. The result appears above the form.

      Use the Roofing Material Calculator when the sloping roof surface area is already measured, such as after measuring the individual roof planes. It estimates roofing squares, bundles, and underlayment rolls without applying another pitch correction.

      Roof Area and Pitch Formulas

      Pitch multiplier = √(1 + (rise / 12)²) = 1 / cos(angle).
      Sloped roof area = horizontal area covered by the roof × pitch multiplier.
      Roofing squares with allowance = ceil(sloped roof area in ft² × 1.10 / 100).

      The house base area is a horizontal area, not the sloped surface. Because a single area does not determine a house's length and width, this tool uses an approximate rectangular footprint with a 2.45:1 length-to-width ratio when adding the eaves. This assumption makes the area and overhang estimate practical without requiring dimensions; for irregular houses, calculate each roof plane separately.

      Worked Example

      For a 2,000 ft² house footprint, a 6/12 pitch and eaves extending 1 ft, the assumed 2.45:1 footprint is approximately 70 × 28.6 ft. The eaves increase both dimensions by 2 ft. Multiply the resulting horizontal covered area by √(1 + (6/12)²) ≈ 1.118 to estimate about 2,461 ft² of sloping roof. The 10% square allowance calls for 28 roofing squares. Actual results depend on the geometry and roof planes.

      Roofing Materials and Allowances

      The material result uses common nominal coverage assumptions: three shingle bundles per 100 ft², roll roofing at 100 ft² per roll, #15 felt at 400 ft² per roll, and #30 felt at 200 ft² per roll. Counts round upward to complete products. It separately reports a 10% buffer in whole roofing squares, like the reference calculator; that buffer is not automatically added to the individual bundle or roll counts.

      Product coverage, overlaps, valleys, ridge caps, fasteners, weather barriers, and local installation rules vary. Check the manufacturer's label and local building code before buying. Roofs with multiple slopes, hips, and dormers should be measured plane by plane.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      What is a roofing square?

      One roofing square equals 100 square feet of sloped roof surface. It is a material-estimating unit, not a square-footage unit for floor plans.

      Do I need the roof's angle and pitch?

      No. Enter the pitch as rise per 12 units, or switch to the Roof Angle tab and enter an angle in degrees. Either determines the same slope factor.

      What if I measured the roof surface directly?

      Use the Roofing Material Calculator. Do not multiply the measured sloping surface by a pitch factor again.

      Why can eaves change the estimate?

      Overhangs cover additional horizontal area beyond the building walls. A single base-area figure does not reveal the exact perimeter, so this estimate assumes a rectangular footprint; actual dimensions improve precision.

      How does the optional price work?

      The price multiplies the estimated sloped roof surface by the selected price-per-area unit. It does not add labor, permits, tear-off, or waste automatically.
