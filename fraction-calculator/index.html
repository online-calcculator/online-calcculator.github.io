How to Use the Fraction Calculator

Choose the calculator that matches your task. For ordinary fraction arithmetic, enter two numerators and denominators, select addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division, and press Calculate. The answer is shown immediately above that calculator's input form, along with simplified terms and calculation steps. Only the most recently calculated section displays a result.

Working with mixed numbers

Use the Mixed Numbers Calculator for numbers such as -2 3/4 and 3 5/7. Enter a whole number followed by a space and a fraction; plain fractions such as 7/8 and whole numbers are also accepted. A minus sign before a mixed number applies to the entire amount.

Simplifying and converting

To simplify a fraction, enter an optional whole-number component plus the numerator and denominator. Decimal to Fraction converts a terminating decimal input into an exact fraction. Fraction to Decimal divides the numerator by the denominator and rounds to 14 decimal places when needed. For very large integers, the Big Number Fraction Calculator performs exact arithmetic using JavaScript BigInt.

How fractions are simplified

Find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of the numerator and denominator and divide both by it. For example, 21/98 is reduced by 7 to 3/14. A mixed number such as 2 21/98 becomes 2 3/14, or 31/14 as an improper fraction.

Decimals and fractions

A terminating decimal may be written over a power of ten: 1.375 = 1375/1000 = 11/8 = 1 3/8. To convert a fraction to decimal form, divide its numerator by its denominator: 2/7 ≈ 0.28571428571429. Repeating decimal expansions are rounded for display, but the stored fraction arithmetic remains exact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a zero denominator not allowed?

Division by zero is undefined. Any fraction with a denominator of zero is rejected with an error message.

Do all six calculators show their results together?

No. When you calculate in one section, any previously visible result in another section is hidden. Inputs in the other sections remain unchanged, and each section has its own Clear button.

How are negative fractions handled?

A negative sign can be placed in the numerator or denominator. Results are normalized so their denominator is positive. For mixed numbers, enter a negative whole-part sign, for example -2 3/4.

Will very large numbers be rounded?

The Big Number Fraction Calculator uses integer arithmetic without floating-point rounding to compute its exact fractional answer. Its optional decimal representation is rounded to 14 places.
