How to Use the Grade Calculator

Enter the assignment or exam name, the grade earned, and the weight assigned to it. A name is optional, but every graded row needs a grade and a weight. Click Calculate to see your current weighted course grade and a breakdown of all included assessments.

By default, you can mix letter grades, percentages, and grade points. Open Settings to choose letter-grade dropdowns, switch from percentage weights to relative weight points, or hide the optional final-grade planning fields. The + add more rows control adds three assignments at a time.

Weighted Grade Formula

The calculator first converts every assignment grade to its grade-point equivalent on the table below. It then computes the weighted average:

Average grade points = Σ(grade points × assignment weight) ÷ Σ(assignment weights)

As an example, suppose homework is 90% with 5% weight, a project is B with 20% weight, and a midterm is 88% with 20% weight. Their equivalents are 3.7, 3.0, and 3.3 grade points. The result is (3.7 × 5 + 3.0 × 20 + 3.3 × 20) ÷ 45 = 3.21 grade points (B+) after rounding. Because the entries total 45%, the average reflects only the work entered, rather than treating the missing 55% as zero.

Final Grade Planning

The optional planner in the first calculator works in grade points: required remaining grade = [goal points × (completed weight + remaining weight) − Σ(completed grade points × weights)] ÷ remaining weight. An entered goal such as A− or 90% is converted to 3.7 points using the scale above. Values above 4.3 grade points cannot be achieved within this scale without extra credit.

Final Exam Grade Formula

For the separate Final Grade Calculator, use a current course grade, target course grade, and percentage of the course grade assigned to the final exam. It calculates:

Required final exam score = (target − current × (1 − final weight)) ÷ final weight, where the weight is expressed as a decimal.

For example, if your current grade is 88%, your goal is 85%, and the final is worth 40%, your required final score is [85 − 88 × 0.60] ÷ 0.40 = 80.5%. This formula assumes the current grade represents all coursework outside the final.

Grading assumptions: Schools use different cutoffs and may apply curving, extra credit, dropped assignments, and category weights. In the final exam calculator, letters are approximated as the middle of their percentage range for a current grade and the lower boundary for a desired grade. Enter exact numerical grades for the most precise estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do assignment weights need to total 100%?

No. The course calculator divides by the sum of the weights you entered. This gives the average for completed work, even if some work remains. For a final-course projection, specify the remaining weight in the optional planner.

What is the difference between percentage weights and weight points?

Percentage weights describe each assignment's contribution to an entire course. Weight points express relative importance: weights of 2 and 3 produce the same weighted average as 40% and 60%. When planning a final grade, the remaining-task weight must use the same format as the completed assignments.

Can I use letter grades with percentages?

Yes. In the default mixed mode, enter grades such as 90, B+, 3.7, or 90%. The grade-point conversion follows the table above. Choose Letters in Settings for grade dropdowns instead.

What if the required final exam grade exceeds 100%?

The result indicates that the stated target is not possible on a 0–100 scale without additional points or extra credit. If your course uses a grade-point scale, the analogous maximum in this calculator is 4.3 points.
