Speed, Distance, and Time

What Is Speed?

Speed tells you how much distance is covered per unit of time. The standard SI unit is the meter per second (m/s), but kilometers per hour (km/h), miles per hour (mph), knots, and feet per second are also common. This calculator treats speed as a non-negative magnitude and assumes a constant rate of travel.

How to Use the Speed Calculator

  1. Select Find Speed, Find Distance, or Find Time.
  2. Enter the two known quantities and choose the desired speed and distance units. For time, fill in hours, minutes, and/or seconds.
  3. Select Calculate to see the answer and steps above the input form. Use Clear to reset the form.
  4. Use the separate Speed Converter to convert between units without changing the calculation above.

Speed, Distance, and Time Formulas

When travel occurs at a constant speed, the three basic equations are:

  • Speed = Distance ÷ Time
  • Distance = Speed × Time
  • Time = Distance ÷ Speed (speed must not be zero).

The calculator first converts distances to meters, speeds to meters per second, and time to seconds. It then solves the selected equation and converts the answer to the selected output unit. Minutes contribute 60 seconds each; hours contribute 3,600 seconds each.

Worked Example: Calculate Speed

Suppose a cyclist covers 100 meters in 20 seconds. Divide 100 by 20 to obtain 5 m/s. Converting to kilometers per hour gives 5 × 3.6 = 18 km/h.

Example for distance: Traveling at 60 mph for 1 hour covers 60 miles. Example for time: At 5 m/s, a distance of 600 m takes 120 seconds, or 2 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert mph to km/h?

Multiply miles per hour by exactly 1.609344. For example, 50 mph is 80.4672 km/h.

What if I leave hours or minutes blank?

Blank time components count as zero. For example, 2 minutes and 30 seconds can be entered without entering hours.

What happens if time or speed is zero?

The calculator rejects division by zero: finding speed requires a positive time; finding time requires a positive speed. Zero distance is allowed and gives zero speed when time is positive.

What is the difference between speed and velocity?

Speed is the magnitude of a rate of travel; velocity includes a direction. These formulas use scalar speed, not directional velocity.
