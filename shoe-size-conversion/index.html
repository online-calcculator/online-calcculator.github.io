How to Use the Shoe Size Converter

Choose Adults, Kids (age 5–12), or Infants/Toddlers (age 0–4). Enter one shoe size in any available regional system, or type your foot length and select centimeters, millimeters, or inches. Select Convert to view the estimated equivalent sizes above the form. Entering a new value clears the other source fields automatically.

For the best estimate, measure both feet while standing, from the back of the heel to the tip of the longest toe, and use the longer foot. Check the footwear brand's width and fit chart before ordering.

How Shoe Size Conversion Is Calculated

Sizing charts are not perfectly standardized internationally. This tool estimates sizes using foot length as a common measurement and rounds to the usual size increments. Set L to foot length in inches and C to foot length in centimeters.

  • US/Canada women's (adult) ≈ 3 × L − 21; men's (adult) ≈ 3 × L − 22.
  • UK/India adult ≈ 3 × L − 23.
  • US/Canada big kids ≈ 3 × L − 22.75; UK/India big kids ≈ 3 × L − 23.75, with the child-size numbering cycle 13.5 → 1.
  • US/Canada infants/toddlers ≈ 3 × L − 9.75; UK/India infants/toddlers ≈ 3 × L − 10.75.
  • European size ≈ 1.5 × C + 2, rounded to a whole EU size.
  • Japan/Mexico ≈ C, rounded to a half size; China ≈ 2 × C − 10, rounded to a whole size.

Length conversions: 1 inch = 2.54 cm = 25.4 mm. US, UK, and Japanese results are rounded to half sizes; EU and China results are rounded to whole sizes. Entering a shoe size reverses the corresponding formula to estimate foot length before converting into other scales. Due to rounding, reversing a shoe size may not reproduce the exact value from a previous conversion.

Example: Converting a 25 cm Foot

A 25 cm foot measures about 9.8425 inches. The approximate US women's size is 3 × 9.8425 − 21 = 8.53 (rounded to 8.5), while the US men's size is about 7.53 (rounded to 7.5), and the UK/India adult size is about 6.53 (rounded to 6.5). The EU estimate is 1.5 × 25 + 2 = 39.5 (rounded to 40); Japan/Mexico is 25, and China is 2 × 25 − 10 = 40.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert from any region rather than foot length?

Yes. Enter a single size in US/Canada, UK/India, EU, Japan/Mexico, or China. The converter estimates the corresponding foot length and then displays the equivalent sizes in the other systems.

Why does a kids' shoe size change from 13.5 to 1?

The traditional child-size scale cycles after 13.5 into youth sizes beginning at 1. The converter handles this separately from adult and infant/toddler sizes.

Is a 25 cm foot always an EU size 40?

It is approximately EU 40 using this converter's rounding method. Because shoes vary in shape and manufacturer sizing, the label and optimal fit can differ.

Are Japan/Mexico and China sizes always standardized?

No. This tool follows the foot-length-based approximations used by the reference chart. Some manufacturers and regions use other labeling conventions, including millimeter-based markings.

Should I size up if I'm between sizes?

Use the brand-specific measurement guide and consider foot width, sock thickness, toe space, and the intended activity. Trying both neighboring sizes when possible is preferable to relying only on a numeric conversion.

Reference and Limitations

Calculation conventions and approximate conversions are based on the Calculator.net Shoe Size Conversion reference. This is a general estimation tool, not an official footwear fitting standard.
