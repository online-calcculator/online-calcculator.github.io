How to Use the Square Footage Calculator

Choose the section matching your surface shape, enter each dimension, and select its unit. Use different units for different dimensions if needed—for example, a length in meters and a width in feet. Enter the number of identical shapes in Quantity and, optionally, a price per unit of area. Press Calculate to see the total area directly above that shape’s input form.

Use Show result in other units to convert the area into square yards, square inches, square meters, acres, and other units. Each section calculates independently, so you can measure several parts of a room or plot separately and then add the areas.

Square Footage Formulas

ShapeFormula for one shapeNotes
RectangleLength × WidthDimensions are measured at right angles.
Rectangle borderL × W − (L − 2b) × (W − 2b)b is the border width measured inward on all four sides.
Circleπ × (diameter ÷ 2)²Enter the full diameter, not the radius.
Ringπ × [R² − (R − b)²]R is half the outer diameter; b is the inward ring width.
Triangle (three edges)√[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)]Heron’s formula, with s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2.
Triangle (base and height)Base × perpendicular height ÷ 2The height must be perpendicular to the base.
Trapezoid(Base 1 + Base 2) × height ÷ 2Height is perpendicular to the parallel bases.
Sectorπ × radius² × angle ÷ 360For degrees; in radians use radius² × angle ÷ 2.
ParallelogramBase × perpendicular heightDo not substitute the sloping side for height.

The calculator converts every length to feet first, evaluates the area formula, and multiplies by Quantity. An optional estimated cost is calculated as total area expressed in the selected pricing unit × price per unit.

Worked Examples

Rectangular room

A room measuring 30 feet by 20 feet covers 600 square feet (30 × 20). At $4 per square foot, the estimated area-based cost would be $2,400, before taxes, wastage, fixtures, or other fees.

Rectangular border

For a 30-foot by 20-foot rectangle with a 2-foot-wide border inside its perimeter, the outer area is 600 square feet, the inside measures 26 × 16 = 416 square feet, and the border covers 184 square feet.

Circular surface

A circle with a diameter of 30 feet has a 15-foot radius. Its area is π × 15², or approximately 706.858 square feet.

Estimating Floor Coverings, Painting and Other Materials

Square footage provides the starting point for estimating flooring, turf, paving, wall finishes, and labor priced by area. For actual purchasing, account for installation waste, cuts, openings, material packaging, and any project-specific measurements. A floor area and a paintable wall area are not necessarily the same measurement.

For L-shaped or irregular surfaces, divide the region into non-overlapping rectangles, triangles, or other supported shapes. Calculate each piece separately and add the resulting square-footage values.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my measurements use different units?

Select the unit beside each measurement. For instance, 3 meters × 10 feet is converted into a consistent unit before the multiplication.

Is the circle input a radius or a diameter?

The Circle calculator requests a diameter, while the Sector calculator requests a radius. The Ring calculator uses an outer diameter and a border width.

Does the calculator handle a quantity of identical shapes?

Yes. Quantity multiplies a single shape’s area. For example, five rectangular panels each measuring 2 × 3 feet cover 30 square feet in total.

How do I calculate the area of a room with an opening?

Calculate the full floor or wall area and separately calculate the opening, then subtract the opening’s area. For a framed rectangular border, use the Rectangle Border section directly.

How is the estimated cost calculated?

When you enter a price, the calculator converts the total area into the selected price denominator (such as square yards) before multiplying by that price. The result is an area-based estimate and does not add taxes, delivery, or labor unless you included those in the quoted unit price.
