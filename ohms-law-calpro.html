How to Use the Ohm's Law Calculator

Your calculated values and the algebraic steps appear immediately above the form. Select Clear to start over, or use the result's save icon to download a text copy.

Ohm's Law and Electrical Power Formulas

For a resistive circuit, Ohm's law connects voltage, current, and resistance: V = I × R. Electrical power is P = V × I. Combining these equations allows any two known quantities to determine the other two, provided the values define a unique finite solution.

Ohm's Law Formula Wheel

Solve Voltage (V)V = I × RV = P ÷ IV = √(P × R)
Solve Current (I)I = V ÷ RI = P ÷ VI = √(P ÷ R)
Solve Resistance (R)R = V ÷ IR = V² ÷ PR = P ÷ I²
Solve Power (P)P = V × IP = I² × RP = V² ÷ R

V is measured in volts (V), I in amperes (A), R in ohms (Ω), and P in watts (W). The calculator converts all inputs into these base units before solving, then converts each result into its selected output unit.

Worked Example: 110 V and 0.2 A

If the voltage is 110 volts and the current is 0.2 amperes, resistance is R = V ÷ I = 110 ÷ 0.2 = 550 Ω. Power is P = V × I = 110 × 0.2 = 22 W. This reproduces the reference example.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate current from resistance and power?

Yes. Enter resistance and power. The calculator uses I = √(P ÷ R) and V = √(P × R) for a passive resistive load.

Why must I provide exactly two values?

These equations normally require two independent known quantities. Providing more values could introduce conflicting measurements. Use exactly two fields, with the remaining fields blank.

Can I enter zero?

Yes, where the chosen pair still gives a unique finite answer—for example, 0 A with a positive resistance results in 0 V and 0 W. Combinations that require division by zero or leave a quantity indeterminate produce a helpful error message.

Does this calculator apply to AC circuits?

The equations directly model DC circuits and purely resistive AC circuits when using consistent RMS values. Reactive AC circuits require impedance and power-factor considerations.
