Results can be saved as a text file using the save control in the green result bar.

What Is a Percentage?

A percentage represents a ratio per hundred. For example, 25 out of 50 is 25 ÷ 50 = 0.5, or 50%. Percentages help compare quantities, evaluate changes, and calculate discounts and markups.

Percentage Formulas

Percentage of a Number

For a percentage rate p, a base value B, and the resulting amount A:

A = (p ÷ 100) × B

To find the percentage when A and B are known, use p = (A ÷ B) × 100. To find the base when A and p are known, use B = A ÷ (p ÷ 100). Division by zero does not give a unique answer.

Percentage Difference

For two positive values that do not have a specific starting or ending order, compare the absolute difference to their average:

Percentage difference = |V₁ − V₂| ÷ ((V₁ + V₂) ÷ 2) × 100

Example: for 12 and 32, the absolute difference is 20 and their average is 22. Thus, 20 ÷ 22 × 100 ≈ 90.9091%. For negative values this tool uses the absolute magnitude of the average so the difference is nonnegative; when the average is zero, the ratio is undefined.

Percentage Increase and Decrease

Increasing a value by p% means multiplying by (1 + p ÷ 100); decreasing it means multiplying by (1 − p ÷ 100). To reverse an increase or decrease, divide the final value by the appropriate multiplier, provided that multiplier is not zero.

The relative change between an initial value and a final value is ((final − initial) ÷ initial) × 100, provided the initial value is not zero.

Worked Examples

  • 5% of 1,000: 1,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = 50.
  • 100 is what percent of 2? 100 ÷ 2 × 100 = 5,000%.
  • 223 increased by 5%: 223 × 1.05 = 234.15.
  • 500 decreased by 10%: 500 × 0.9 = 450.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between percentage change and percentage difference?

Percentage change measures movement relative to a known starting value, so its sign indicates a rise or fall. Percentage difference treats two values symmetrically, comparing their distance to their average.

Can I calculate a missing original value?

Yes. In the first and percentage change calculators, leave the original value blank and supply the other two known values. The tool solves the reverse equation where the answer is mathematically unique.

Why is a percentage sometimes undefined?

Some calculations require division by a base value, percentage, or multiplier. If that divisor is zero, the missing value may be impossible or not uniquely determined. The calculator explains these cases instead of displaying Infinity or NaN.

Does the calculator support decimal and negative numbers?

Yes. Decimal numbers, signs, and scientific notation are supported. Be careful interpreting percentage differences involving negative values, because different mathematical conventions exist.
