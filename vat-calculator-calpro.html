How to Use the VAT Calculator

Enter any two of these four numbers: the VAT rate, net price (before VAT), gross price (including VAT), or tax amount. Leave the other two boxes empty, then select Calculate. The missing values will appear in the green result panel above the input form, together with a doughnut chart showing how the final price is split between the net amount and VAT.

For example, if you know the VAT rate and the price before tax, enter those two values to add VAT. If you have a receipt with the final price and the VAT rate, enter those values to remove VAT. You can also determine the percentage rate from a price and tax amount.

Use Clear to empty all four fields and start another calculation. The print button in the result header opens your browser's print dialog, where you can print or save the result as a PDF.

VAT Calculation Formulas

In the formulas below, r is the VAT percentage expressed as a decimal: for a 20% tax rate, r = 0.20.

FindFormula
Tax amountVAT = Net price × r
Gross priceGross = Net price × (1 + r) = Net + VAT
Net priceNet = Gross price ÷ (1 + r)
VAT rateVAT rate (%) = (VAT amount ÷ Net price) × 100
VAT from grossVAT = Gross price − [Gross price ÷ (1 + r)]

Worked Example: 20% VAT on 1,200

Suppose a product has a net price of 1,200 and a VAT rate of 20%. The VAT amount is 1,200 × 0.20 = 240, and the gross price is 1,200 + 240 = 1,440. These are the results shown by the calculator's default example when you select Calculate.

To reverse the calculation, divide a VAT-inclusive price of 1,440 by 1.20. The original net amount is 1,200; subtracting it from 1,440 gives 240 in VAT. The VAT is calculated as 20% of the net price, not 20% of the VAT-inclusive gross price.

Understanding Your Results

Net price vs. gross price

The net price is the amount before VAT. Gross price includes VAT, and the difference between gross and net is the tax amount. The chart shows net price in blue and tax amount in green, with the gross total in the center.

Rounding and currency

Calculations use unrounded values internally; displayed monetary values are rounded to at most two decimal places and VAT percentages to at most six decimal places. Enter amounts in the currency of your choice and keep that currency consistent across all price fields. This tool does not convert between currencies or determine which VAT rate legally applies.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Enter the gross (VAT-inclusive) price and the VAT rate, leaving the net price and tax amount empty. The calculator divides the gross price by 1 plus the decimal VAT rate to find the original net price.

Can I work out the VAT percentage?

Yes. Enter net price plus gross price, net price plus tax amount, or gross price plus tax amount. The rate is the tax amount divided by the net price, multiplied by 100.

Can the VAT rate be 0%?

Yes. A 0% rate and a known net or gross price uniquely determine the remaining amounts, with VAT equal to zero. Some pairs of all-zero inputs cannot establish a unique rate or price; the calculator explains when more information is required.

What if my input values disagree?

Only two values are needed. If you enter three or four values, they must be mathematically consistent within normal price rounding. Clear or correct any conflicting figures and recalculate.

Does this calculator determine my VAT obligation?

No. Actual tax rules, exemptions, rates, and invoice rounding can vary by jurisdiction and transaction. Use the output for arithmetic estimates and verify tax treatment with the relevant tax authority.
