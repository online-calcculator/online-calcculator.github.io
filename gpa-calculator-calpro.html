How to use the GPA calculator

Enter each course's credit hours and the grade earned, then select Calculate. Add more courses as needed. Open Settings to enter grades as letters, percentages, or numerical grade points, add past GPA and credits, or divide your courses into semesters. Courses marked P, NP, I, or W are excluded from the GPA calculation.

The GPA report appears above the course-entry form, with a breakdown of every counted course, total credits, quality points, and overall GPA. The separate GPA Planning Calculator can help determine the average grade points required in future classes to reach a target GPA.

GPA formula and worked example

GPA is a credit-weighted average. Multiply each course's grade-point value by its credit hours, add the results, and divide by the sum of GPA-counted credit hours:

GPA = Σ (course credits × course grade points) ÷ Σ (counted course credits)

Suppose Math earns an A (4.0) for 3 credits, English earns a B+ (3.3) for 3 credits, and History earns an A− (3.7) for 2 credits. The total quality points are (3 × 4.0) + (3 × 3.3) + (2 × 3.7) = 29.3. The total credits are 8, giving 29.3 ÷ 8 = 3.6625, displayed as 3.663.

For percentage input, this implementation maps grades to the common U.S. bands: 97–100 A+, 93–96.99 A, 90–92.99 A−, 87–89.99 B+, continuing downward in approximately three-point intervals, and below 60 F. These cutoffs are a convention rather than a universal grading rule.

Semester GPA versus cumulative GPA

Semester GPA includes only the current semester's counted courses. If you enable Add GPA of prior semesters, the calculator combines the new quality points with your previous record:

Cumulative GPA = (previous GPA × previous credits + new course quality points) ÷ (previous credits + new counted credits)

Grouping courses by semester also provides a semester-by-semester breakdown. Courses marked pass, non-pass, incomplete, or withdrawal do not contribute to either quality points or GPA-counted credits in this tool.

GPA planning formula

To calculate the average GPA required over additional credits, use the current GPA, completed credits, desired cumulative GPA, and planned credits:

Required future GPA = [target GPA × (completed credits + future credits) − current GPA × completed credits] ÷ future credits

For example, with a current GPA of 3.663 across 8 credits, a target GPA of 3.000, and 15 future credits, the required future GPA is 2.646. If the answer is higher than your school's maximum grade-point value, the target is mathematically unattainable over only those additional credits.

Frequently asked questions

Does a course with more credits affect GPA more?

Yes. The same grade in a four-credit course contributes twice the quality points of a two-credit course. Credit hours determine its weighting in the GPA calculation.

Does a pass/fail or withdrawal affect my GPA?

In this calculator, P, NP, I, and W courses are excluded. Some institutions handle these statuses differently, including for earned-credit requirements; your transcript policy takes precedence.

Can I calculate a weighted AP or honors GPA?

Yes, if your institution provides weighted point values, switch to Point Value and enter the correct value per course. For example, a weighted A might be worth 5.0 points at some schools. The calculator does not assume a universal AP/IB bonus.

Why is my school's GPA slightly different?

Potential reasons include different A+ values, grade conversion boundaries, retake rules, excluded-course policies, or rounding. For official academic decisions, consult your school's grade policy and transcript.

How many decimal places does the result display?

Results show three decimal places, rounded only after all quality points have been summed. The calculator uses unrounded grades internally.
