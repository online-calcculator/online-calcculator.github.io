How to Use the Day Counter

Use the first calculator to find the number of days between two dates. Select each month, day, and year or click the calendar icon. By default, the start date is counted and the end date is excluded. Select Include end day to include both endpoints.

Click Settings to select common U.S. holidays and add recurring custom holidays. The result displays the elapsed days, weekdays, weekend days, selected holiday dates, and weekdays remaining after selected holidays are excluded.

Count Days from a Date

Use the second calculator to move forward or backward by a chosen number of days. Enter an integer, choose + or −, and click Calculate. Select Count business days only to skip Saturdays and Sundays. In its settings, you can also skip selected holidays.

The starting date is not counted when adding or subtracting days. For example, adding 15 calendar days to November 4, 2026 returns November 19, 2026.

Day Counting Formulas

Calendar Days Between Two Dates

Days = |End date − Start date| + E, where dates are converted to integer calendar-day numbers and E is 1 when Include end day is checked, otherwise 0. Leap years and varying month lengths are handled by the Gregorian calendar. Reversed date ranges return a positive count.

Weekdays and Business Days

Each complete seven-day week has five weekdays and two weekend days, assuming a Monday–Friday workweek. The calculator counts the remaining partial week separately. Business days are weekdays that do not fall on any of the selected holidays:

Business days = Monday–Friday dates − selected holiday dates falling Monday–Friday. A holiday falling on a weekend is not deducted twice.

Adding and Subtracting Days

For calendar-day calculations, the target date is the start date plus or minus the selected number of days. For business-day calculations, the calculator walks forward or backward, counting only eligible weekdays until the requested count is reached.

Holiday Counting and Observance

The holiday list follows familiar U.S. date rules: for instance, Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November, and Memorial Day is the last Monday of May. Fixed holidays are counted on their listed calendar dates. If an organization observes a different date when a holiday falls on a weekend, add the observed date as a custom holiday. Federal, state, school, and employer holiday schedules may differ.

For official U.S. federal holiday schedules and observed dates, consult the U.S. Office of Personnel Management.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the day counter include the start date?

Yes. For date differences, the default count includes the starting date and excludes the ending date. Checking Include end day adds one day to the total.

Are weekends counted as holidays?

Weekends and holidays are reported separately. If a selected holiday falls on Saturday or Sunday, it is not subtracted again when computing working days.

What happens when the start and end dates are the same?

The answer is 0 days by default, or 1 day when Include end day is selected.

Can I subtract business days?

Yes. Choose the minus sign, enter the number of days, select the business-day option, and adjust holiday settings if necessary.

Does the calculator adjust for different time zones or daylight saving time?

No time-of-day is entered. Calculations use whole Gregorian calendar dates, so they are not affected by daylight-saving transitions or the browser’s time zone during calculation.
