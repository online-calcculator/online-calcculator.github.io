How to Use the Average Calculator

Enter a list of numbers in the calculator above, separating values with commas, spaces, or line breaks. Click Calculate to display the arithmetic average and its step-by-step calculation. Edit the numbers and calculate again, or select Clear to empty the form. The Save control downloads your current results as a plain-text file.

Average Formula

The arithmetic mean (often called the average) is the sum of all values divided by the number of values. Each observation is weighted equally:

Average = (x₁ + x₂ + ... + xₙ) / n = Sum / Count

Worked Example

For the seven numbers 10, 2, 38, 23, 38, 23, 21, the sum is 155 and the count is 7. The average is 155 ÷ 7 = 22.142857142857 (shown to about 14 significant digits). The median is 23, the smallest value is 2, the largest is 38, and the range is 36.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the average the same as the median?

No. The average depends on the sum of every value; the median depends on their sorted positions. Very large or small values can pull the average away from the median.

Can I use negative numbers and decimals?

Yes. For example, entering -3, 0, 1.5, 5.5 gives an average of 1. The conventional geometric mean is not shown for a data set with negative values.

Does this calculator include weighted averages?

No. Each entered value contributes equally to the arithmetic mean. A weighted average requires a weight for each value.

Why does the answer have several decimal places?

Some divisions create repeating decimals. The calculator displays up to approximately 14 significant digits, so long answers are rounded for readability.
