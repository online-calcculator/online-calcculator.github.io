How to Use the Tip Calculator

Enter the price of a meal or service in the first calculator and choose Calculate. The table shows tips from 5% to 50% and the corresponding total amounts; the 15% row is emphasized for easy comparison. Use Clear to empty the form and hide the results.

For a group, use the Shared Bill Tip Calculator. Enter the bill before tip, a tip percentage and the number of people. The results above that form show the tip and overall amount per person. Both calculators use U.S. dollars and round displayed money to two decimal places. You can save calculated results as a CSV using the result header's Save button.

Tip Calculation Formula

Tip amount = bill amount × (tip percentage ÷ 100).

Total bill = bill amount + tip amount.

Tip per person = total tip ÷ number of people. Total per person = total bill ÷ number of people.

Worked Example: $55 Bill and 15% Tip

A $55.00 bill at 15% yields $8.25 in tip ($55 × 0.15), for a total of $63.25. Shared by two people, each person's tip is approximately $4.13 and each person's total is approximately $31.63. Rounding may leave one cent that needs to be assigned to one person.

Check your receipt: If a service charge or gratuity is already added, calculate any additional tip based on what you actually intend to leave. Local customs and tax treatment vary.

Tipping Customs Around the World

Tipping practices vary widely. Percentages may be customary in one country, optional in another, or inappropriate in some settings. The following geographic illustration presents only selected broad examples and is not a universal rulebook.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should the tip be calculated before tax?

A commonly used U.S. convention is to calculate a percentage on the pretax service price. If your receipt shows tax separately, enter the pretax amount when following that convention.

What if I want to tip a different percentage?

The shared-bill form accepts a custom percentage, including decimal values such as 17.5%. The first calculator uses fixed comparison rates of 5%, 10%, 12%, 14%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 25%, 30% and 50%.

How are cents divided in a group?

All values are rounded to the nearest cent. When an amount cannot divide evenly across the group, one or more people may need to pay a cent more or less so that the final combined payment matches the bill.

Does the calculator include taxes or discounts?

No. It treats the amount entered as the base price for tipping. Apply any applicable discounts or choose whether to include tax before entering the price.
