How to Use the VA Mortgage Calculator

Enter the home purchase price, down payment (as a percentage or dollar amount), loan term, and fixed annual interest rate. Select whether this is your first VA loan and whether you qualify for a VA funding-fee exemption. Choose to pay the funding fee at closing or finance it into the loan, then select Calculate.

Use More Options to add annual property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, and other recurring costs. You can model yearly expense increases, recurring extra principal payments, a one-time prepayment, and an illustrative biweekly payment schedule. The annual and monthly amortization tabs show how much each period's payment goes toward interest and principal.

VA Mortgage Payment Formula

For a fixed-rate loan, the monthly principal-and-interest payment is:

M = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n)

Here P is the loan principal including a financed VA funding fee, r is the annual interest rate divided by 12, and n is the total number of monthly payments. For a 0% annual interest rate, the payment is simply P ÷ n. The VA funding fee is calculated against the purchase loan amount before the fee is financed.

Worked Example

Consider a $500,000 purchase with no down payment, a 30-year term, 7.085% interest, and first-time VA loan use with no funding-fee exemption. The applicable 2.15% funding fee is $10,750. If financed, the opening mortgage balance becomes $510,750. The estimated monthly principal-and-interest payment is $3,427.24. Over 360 payments, the estimated total is $1,233,805.99, including approximately $723,055.99 in interest. Optional taxes and insurance are not included in these figures.

VA Funding Fee Rates for Home Purchases

The standard VA purchase-loan funding fee depends on the down payment and whether the VA benefit has been used before. VA currently publishes these rates for both active-duty borrowers and National Guard/Reserve borrowers.

Down PaymentFirst UseSubsequent Use
Less than 5%2.15%3.30%
5% to less than 10%1.50%1.50%
10% or more1.25%1.25%

Qualifying borrowers may be exempt from the funding fee, including veterans receiving eligible service-connected disability compensation, certain surviving spouses receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, and other categories identified by VA. The exemption is not based only on having a 10% disability rating. Confirm the determination with your lender or VA before closing.

Understanding the Results

The green payment headline reports principal and interest only when optional costs are disabled. With More Options enabled, it reports the first month's estimated housing payment, including the optional costs you entered. Amortization rows show the interest charged during the period, the principal paid (including extra payments), and the balance after payment. Costs entered under More Options may rise annually according to your increase assumptions.

Early extra principal payments can reduce both the mortgage payoff period and cumulative interest. The optional biweekly scenario is an illustration based on 26 half-monthly payments per year; actual lenders may use different posting schedules, escrow practices, and interest-accrual rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a VA loan require a down payment?

Eligible borrowers can often get a VA-backed purchase loan with no down payment, subject to lender underwriting and VA requirements. A down payment can reduce the loan principal and potentially the funding-fee rate.

Can I finance the VA funding fee?

Yes. VA permits the funding fee for a purchase loan to be included in the mortgage balance, increasing the principal and interest paid over time. Other purchase closing costs generally must be paid separately.

Does the VA loan require monthly mortgage insurance?

No. Standard VA-backed purchase loans do not require private mortgage insurance (PMI). Property taxes, homeowners insurance, and HOA charges may still apply.

What if my disability rating does not meet 10%?

VA funding-fee exemption rules refer to qualifying compensation eligibility and several other statutory categories; a blanket 10% minimum is not the appropriate test. Ask VA or your lender to verify your exemption status.

Are the rates fixed for every type of VA loan?

No. The fee-rate table above applies to VA-backed purchase loans. Cash-out refinances, streamline refinances, assumptions, and certain other loan types have different fee structures.
