How to Use the Discount Calculator

Enter any two known values and leave the remaining fields empty. Choose Percent off for a percentage discount or Fixed amount off for a dollar reduction. Select Calculate to see the sale price and your savings directly above the form. You can also use any two known values to find the original price or discount. Select Clear to start a new calculation.

Discount Calculator Formulas

Percentage Discounts

For original price P and percentage discount r:

  • Amount saved = P × (r ÷ 100)
  • Price after discount = P − amount saved = P × (1 − r ÷ 100)
  • Original price = final price ÷ (1 − r ÷ 100), provided r is less than 100%
  • Discount percentage = (amount saved ÷ original price) × 100, for an original price greater than zero

Fixed-Amount Discounts

For a fixed dollar discount D: final price = original price − D. Conversely, original price = final price + D, and D = original price − final price.

Worked Examples

15% off $59.99: The unrounded saving is $59.99 × 0.15 = $8.9985, shown as $9.00. The final price is $59.99 − $8.9985 = $50.9915, shown as $50.99.

$20 off $95: Select Fixed amount off, enter 95 as the original price and 20 as the discount. The final price is $75.00, and the saving is $20.00.

Understanding the Results

The result shows the price to pay after the discount and the amount saved. Dollar amounts are displayed to two decimal places; calculations retain precision until the final display. Sales tax, shipping, coupon exclusions, and successive/stacked discounts are not included automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate an unknown original price?

Yes. In percentage mode, provide the percentage discount and final price. For example, a $75 final price after 25% off implies a $100 original price. A 100% discount with only the final price does not uniquely determine the original price.

How do I calculate a discount rate from two prices?

Enter the original and final prices and leave the discount field empty. The calculator uses (original price − final price) ÷ original price × 100.

Why does the calculator request exactly two values?

These discount formulas solve the remaining fields from two independent known values. Clearing the other fields avoids conflicting or ambiguous inputs. Some combinations involving all-zero prices or 0%/100% discounts cannot produce a unique answer, and the calculator explains those cases.

Does this calculator include sales tax?

No. The amounts shown are pre-tax unless the entered amounts already include tax.
