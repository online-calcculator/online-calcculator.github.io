How to Use the Surface Area Calculator

Select the shape you need and enter the lengths shown in its input panel. Choose the correct unit for each dimension, then click Calculate. The corresponding result appears directly above that shape’s form. The calculator converts mixed input units automatically and reports total surface area in square units based on the first measurement’s unit. Use Clear to reset only that shape.

All results refer to the external area of a closed solid, unless specifically identified as a curved or lateral component. An open-top container requires subtracting its missing top area from the closed-solid total.

Surface Area Formulas for Each Shape

Sphere (Ball)

A sphere with radius r has surface area A = 4πr². For a radius of 5 cm, A = 100π ≈ 314.159 cm².

Cone

For a right circular cone, the total area is A = πr² + πr√(r² + h²), with the first term for the base and the second for the sloping side. The slant height is √(r² + h²).

Cube

A cube has six equal square faces: A = 6a², where a is the edge length. A cube with a 4-inch edge has 96 in² of surface area.

Cylindrical Tank

For a closed cylinder, A = 2πr² + 2πrh. The first term includes the two circular ends; the second represents the curved wall. To calculate an open-top tank, subtract πr² from the total.

Rectangular Tank

A rectangular prism with length l, width w, and height h has A = 2(lw + lh + wh). For a 3 × 4 × 5 ft box, the surface area is 94 ft².

Capsule

A capsule consists of a cylinder with a hemisphere at each end. The total external surface area is A = 4πr² + 2πrh, where h is only the straight cylinder length. The full end-to-end capsule height is h + 2r.

Spherical Cap

For ball radius R, cap height h, and flat-base radius r, the curved surface is 2πRh, and the circular base is πr². The calculator reports their sum: A = 2πRh + πr². Since r² = 2Rh − h², you can enter any two dimensions to solve for the third. When only r and R are provided, this calculator assumes the smaller cap.

Conical Frustum

The total area of a closed right conical frustum is A = π(R² + r²) + π(R + r)√((R − r)² + h²). The two circular ends and the sloped wall are shown separately.

Ellipsoid

An ellipsoid has no simple elementary general surface-area formula. This calculator uses the Knud Thomsen approximation: A ≈ 4π[((ab)p + (ac)p + (bc)p) / 3]1/p, with p = 1.6 to follow the reference calculator. Here a, b, and c are semi-axes, not full diameters. Accuracy can vary depending on the ellipsoid proportions.

Square Pyramid

For square base edge a and perpendicular height h, A = a² + 2a√((a/2)² + h²). The first term is the square base; the second is the combined area of four triangular sides.

How the Results Work

The largest result is the total area. Whenever a solid has distinct faces or curved parts, the calculator also reports the component areas. All components and output conversions use the same underlying dimensions in meters, so different input units can be combined without manual conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between surface area and volume?

Surface area measures the outside boundary of an object in square units, while volume measures the space inside it in cubic units.

Can I enter radius in centimeters and height in feet?

Yes. Each input has its own unit dropdown. All values are converted before the formula is evaluated.

Does a cylindrical tank result include the lid?

Yes. This calculator reports the total area of a closed tank, including its top, bottom, and curved side. Subtract one circular end for an open-top tank.

Is the curved surface alone returned for a spherical cap?

No. The total includes both the curved dome and the circular base; the separate component areas are also reported.

Are ellipsoid results exact?

No. The ellipsoid formula is an approximation with exponent 1.6, matching the supplied reference. For high-precision scientific or engineering work, use numerical methods or an exact special-case equation where applicable.
