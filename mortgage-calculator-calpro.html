How to Use the Mortgage Calculator

Enter the purchase price, the amount or percentage of your down payment, your mortgage term, annual fixed interest rate, and your first payment month. Select Calculate to estimate principal and interest, the total monthly cost of owning the home, and a full amortization schedule. A sample scenario is already calculated when the page opens.

Enable Include Taxes & Costs Below to include annual property tax, homeowners insurance, private mortgage insurance (PMI), HOA fees, and other annual housing expenses. Switch each field between a percentage and an annual dollar amount. Select More Options to model annual increases in non-PMI costs, extra monthly or yearly principal payments, or one-time principal prepayments.

Switch between the Annual Schedule and Monthly Schedule to inspect how each loan payment reduces the balance. Save a text summary, export the amortization table as CSV, or print the results directly from this page.

Mortgage Payment Formula

For a fixed-rate, fully amortizing mortgage, the scheduled monthly principal-and-interest payment is calculated as:

M = P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)−n]

Where P is the original loan principal (price minus down payment), r is the annual interest rate divided by 1,200, and n is the number of monthly payments (years × 12). If the interest rate is zero, M = P ÷ n.

For each month, interest equals the remaining loan balance multiplied by the monthly rate. Scheduled principal equals the mortgage payment minus that month's interest. Extra principal payments are applied to the balance immediately and can reduce the total interest and loan term. The last installment is adjusted to the amount still owed.

Worked Example: $500,000 Home

With a $500,000 house price, 20% ($100,000) down, a $400,000 loan, a 7.158% fixed interest rate, and a 30-year term, the scheduled principal and interest is approximately $2,703.79 per month. If property tax is 1.2% of the house price ($6,000 per year), insurance is $1,500 per year, and other costs are $4,000 per year, estimated first-month out-of-pocket expenses total approximately $3,662.12, excluding any separate upfront closing costs.

Understanding Your Results

Principal and Interest Versus Total Housing Cost

The large green monthly-payment figure is the principal-and-interest payment, not a complete monthly homeownership budget. The breakdown below it adds the other costs you choose to include. Escrowed tax or insurance contributions and separate HOA payments may differ depending on the lender and property.

PMI and Annual Increases

The PMI field is an optional annual estimate. This calculator estimates PMI only on loans initially below a 20% down payment, stopping it once the projected balance reaches 78% of the original property price; your actual cancellation terms may differ. Annual percentage increases in property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA, and other costs are applied once every 12 payments. Results use the entered annual amounts and do not forecast local tax assessments or insurance quotes.

Extra Payments and Biweekly Comparison

Extra payments reduce principal, rather than the interest rate, and may shorten the payoff period. The optional biweekly comparison models 26 half-sized P&I payments per year using a simplified 26-period interest model; it is calculated separately from optional extra payments and housing costs, so it is a planning comparison rather than a lender-specific repayment schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a larger down payment lower monthly costs?

Generally, yes. A larger down payment decreases your loan principal and may eliminate the need for PMI. The property tax and other homeowner costs do not necessarily fall with the loan amount.

Does this calculator support adjustable-rate mortgages?

No. The amortization schedule assumes the entered annual interest rate remains fixed for the entire term. Adjustable-rate loans require separate assumptions for rate resets, caps, and payment changes.

Does it include closing costs or a down payment in total out-of-pocket costs?

The lifetime out-of-pocket estimate includes mortgage principal and interest plus selected recurring housing expenses. It does not include the original down payment, closing costs, utilities not entered as other costs, capital gains, property appreciation, or investment opportunity costs.

Are the displayed totals exact lender figures?

No. Values are mathematically modeled estimates; real escrow payments, PMI policy, amortization conventions, and dates may differ from your lender's disclosures. Displayed monthly amounts are rounded to cents, while the underlying calculation retains full precision.
