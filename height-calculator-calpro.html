How tall will my child be?

Adult height depends on many factors, including inherited characteristics, age, nutrition, overall health, and the timing of puberty. The first calculator gives a statistical estimate from a child’s age, sex, current height and weight, and the heights of both parents. Estimates can differ from eventual adult height.

How to Use the Height Calculator

  1. Enter the child’s age in decimal years (for example, 5.5 is five years and six months), and choose male or female.
  2. Enter the child’s height, weight, and both parents’ heights using US, metric, or other units.
  3. Select Calculate to see the predicted adult height above the form.
  4. If the child is younger than four, try the parents-only estimate instead.
  5. Use the height converter to calculate feet/inches from centimeters or vice versa.

Height Prediction Formulas

Khamis–Roche regression method

For ages 4 to 17.5, the calculator uses the age- and sex-specific Khamis–Roche coefficients. The calculation is performed in inches and pounds:

Predicted adult height (in) = β₀ + β₁ × child height (in) + β₂ × child weight (lb) + β₃ × mean parental height (in)

Mean parental height is (mother’s height + father’s height) ÷ 2. Coefficients are drawn from the original research with corrections published in 1995. To mirror the reference calculator’s intermediate-age presentation, each surrounding half-year prediction is rounded to whole inches before interpolation. This output-format convention is not a new scientific model.

Parents-only estimate (mid-parental height)

For a boy: (mother’s height + father’s height) ÷ 2 + 2.5 inches. For a girl: (mother’s height + father’s height) ÷ 2 − 2.5 inches. This simpler estimate is useful when current child measurements are not available, but it has substantial individual uncertainty.

Worked Example

A child aged 5.2 years, 3 feet 8 inches tall and weighing 40 pounds, with a mother 5 feet 5 inches and father 5 feet 10 inches, has an illustrative age-based prediction of 5 feet 10.6 inches (179.32 cm) using the reference-style half-year rounding and interpolation. For parents measuring 5 feet 2 inches and 5 feet 10 inches, the parents-only estimates are 5 feet 8.5 inches for a boy and 5 feet 3.5 inches for a girl.

Height Conversion Formulas

Centimeters = (12 × feet + inches) × 2.54. To convert centimeters to feet and inches, divide centimeters by 2.54 to obtain total inches, then split into complete feet and the remaining inches. For example, 5 feet 5 inches is 165.10 cm, and 178 cm equals 5 feet and approximately 10.08 inches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I predict height for children under four?

The age-specific Khamis–Roche regression is tabulated from age 4. For younger children, select the parents-only calculation. It is not equivalent to a clinical assessment.

Is this a guaranteed adult height?

No. Growth variation, puberty timing and health conditions can all shift the final outcome. If growth is unusually slow or changes markedly on a growth chart, ask a qualified health professional to assess it.

Why is the height prediction different from the parents-only result?

The regression model uses the child’s present height, weight and age in addition to parent heights; the simpler calculation considers the parents only. Different inputs and methods naturally produce different estimates.
