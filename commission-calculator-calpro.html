How to Use the Commission Calculator

Use the simple commission calculator when commission is a single percentage of sales. Fill in any two of Sales Price, Commission Rate, and Commission; leave the quantity you want to find empty. Select Calculate to see the answer and substitution steps above the form.

For a compensation plan with several rates, use the Tiered Commission Calculator. Enter total sales, select whether there is a base payment, and choose whether the rate changes as sales increase. Up to eight tiers can be configured with upper sales limits and percentage or fixed-dollar commission amounts.

Commission Formulas

Simple percentage commission

The base relationship is Commission = Sales price × Rate ÷ 100. Reversing it gives Rate (%) = Commission ÷ Sales price × 100 and Sales price = Commission ÷ (Rate ÷ 100). For example, a $200,000 sale at 3% earns $6,000.

Progressive tiered commission

For progressive tiers, a rate applies only to the sales amount inside its range. This differs from a flat rate applied to the entire sales total once a target is reached. For each tier, calculate the eligible sales, multiply them by that tier's percentage, and add the resulting commissions.

With $27,000 of sales, a first tier of 3% on $0–$20,000, a second tier of 5% on $20,000–$25,000, and a third tier of 10% above $25,000 produces $600 + $250 + $200 = $1,050. On $200,000 of sales with 3% through $20,000 and 5% above $20,000, the total is $600 + $9,000 = $9,600.

Base payments and flat-dollar commission

When a base commission is enabled, it is added to the sales-based commission. A base entered in dollars adds that amount directly; a base entered as a percentage adds that percentage of total sales. When commission does not vary with price, choose one percentage of sales or one fixed-dollar payment. Within the tier table, a dollar-denominated tier is treated as a one-time amount when sales enter that tier—not a rate applied to every dollar in the tier.

Reading Your Results

The simple result shows the missing value and its formula steps. The tiered result shows total commission and a breakdown for each tier that contributed to it, plus the base amount if one is selected. All dollar results display up to two decimal places, while calculations retain full numeric precision until final presentation. Frequently Asked Questions

Is tiered commission the same as applying the highest rate to all sales?

No. This calculator uses a progressive, or marginal, tier structure: reaching a new rate does not change the earnings already accrued in lower tiers.

What do “To” and “From” mean?

“From” is calculated automatically from the preceding row's “To” amount. Enter an increasing “To” limit for each non-final tier, and leave the last “To” blank for an unlimited final tier. Fill tiers in order without leaving a gap.

Can I calculate commission from a payout amount?

Yes. In the simple calculator, enter the commission and either the sales price or the percentage; leave the unknown value blank. A zero sales price cannot determine a rate, and a zero rate cannot determine sales price from commission.

Does this calculator estimate taxes or take-home pay?

No. The result is gross commission before taxes, deductions, clawbacks, or employer-specific adjustments. The base amount is an additional commission payment, not an annual salary schedule.
