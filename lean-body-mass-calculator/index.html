How to Use the Lean Body Mass Calculator

Choose Metric Units for centimeters and kilograms, US Units for feet, inches, and pounds, or Other Units to enter meters, millimeters, ounces, stone, and additional units. Select your sex and indicate whether the person is 14 years old or younger. Enter height and weight, then select Calculate. Changing the unit tab converts your measurements without changing the underlying values.

The results appear beside the input form on desktop screens and below it on smaller screens. The Save icon exports the current comparison table as a text file. Clear empties the form so you can start again.

Lean Body Mass Formulas

Lean body mass is the portion of total body weight that is not body fat. The equations here estimate lean mass from sex, height, and weight; they do not directly measure body composition. For all equations, W means weight in kilograms and H means height in centimeters.

Boer formula (adults)

Male: LBM = 0.407 × W + 0.267 × H − 19.2
Female: LBM = 0.252 × W + 0.473 × H − 48.3

James formula (adults)

Male: LBM = 1.10 × W − 128 × (W/H)2
Female: LBM = 1.07 × W − 148 × (W/H)2

Hume formula (adults)

Male: LBM = 0.32810 × W + 0.33929 × H − 29.5336
Female: LBM = 0.29569 × W + 0.41813 × H − 43.2933

Peters formula (children aged 14 or younger)

Estimated extracellular volume: eECV = 0.0215 × W0.6469 × H0.7236. Estimated lean body mass: LBM = 3.8 × eECV. The calculator displays Peters in place of the three adult formulas when you select the younger-age option.

Worked Example

For an adult male who is 180 cm tall and weighs 60 kg, Boer gives 53.3 kg of lean body mass (about 89% of body weight), James gives 51.8 kg (86%), and Hume gives 51.2 kg (85%). The corresponding implied body-fat percentages are approximately 11%, 14%, and 15%.

The table calculates implied body-fat percentage as (1 − LBM / total weight) × 100. These percentages are model outputs rather than measured body-fat percentages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is lean body mass the same as muscle mass?

No. Lean body mass includes muscle, water, bone, blood, organs, and other non-fat tissues. It is not a measurement of muscle alone.

Why do the Boer, James, and Hume results differ?

They are different regression equations developed from different populations and assumptions. Because they estimate rather than measure body composition, a difference between results is expected.

Is this suitable for children?

The calculator uses the Peters equation for ages 14 or younger. Pediatric lean-mass estimation requires age-appropriate methods, and this number should not be used on its own for clinical decisions.

Can I enter pounds or stone?

Yes. Select US Units for pounds or Other Units for kilograms, pounds, ounces, grams, or stone. All computations use the equivalent weight in kilograms and height in centimeters internally.

Can an estimate imply a negative body-fat percentage?

At unusual height-to-weight combinations, a regression formula can yield a lean-mass estimate greater than total weight. The calculator shows the computed result and flags this limitation instead of silently changing the formula.
