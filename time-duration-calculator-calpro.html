How to Use the Time Duration Calculator

For the time-only calculator, enter a start hour, minute, second and AM/PM, then an end time. Select Calculate to see the difference broken down into hours, minutes and seconds, along with totals converted to each unit. The Now controls fill a time with your device's current local clock time. The swap control reverses the clock times.

For longer intervals, use Time Between Two Dates. Select the start and end calendar dates and enter the two times. The calendar controls use a month, day and year selector and a native date picker. Both result panels appear above their respective forms, without leaving the page.

Time Duration Formula

First convert each 12-hour time to 24-hour time. For a PM hour other than 12, add 12; for 12 AM, use hour 0. The number of seconds since midnight is:

Total seconds = (hours × 3,600) + (minutes × 60) + seconds.

Subtract the start from the end. In time-only mode, if the result is negative, add 86,400 seconds to represent an end time on the following day. In date-and-time mode, combine the calendar-day difference with the difference between the clock times. This implementation measures calendar-based elapsed time and does not adjust for time zones or daylight-saving transitions.

Worked Examples

From 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM on the same day: 17:30 − 08:30 = 9 hours, or 540 minutes and 32,400 seconds.

From 11:45 PM to 1:15 AM the next day: 15 minutes before midnight plus 75 minutes after midnight = 1 hour 30 minutes.

Understanding the Results

The first output is the duration in complete days, hours, minutes and seconds (or hours, minutes and seconds in time-only mode). Below it, totals in days, hours, minutes and seconds make conversion and comparison easier. Fractional day and hour totals are rounded to at most four decimal places for display; calculations are performed using whole seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the end time is earlier than the start time?

For time-only entries, CalcPro assumes the end is on the next day. For entries with dates, the calculator reports the positive, absolute interval and notes when the entered dates are reversed.

Does 12 AM mean midnight or noon?

12 AM is midnight (00:00) and 12 PM is noon (12:00). All other PM hours are twelve hours ahead of their AM counterparts on a 24-hour clock.

Does this calculator account for daylight saving time?

No. The dated calculator compares the entered calendar dates and local clock times without a time-zone setting. Across a daylight-saving change, actual elapsed seconds may differ by one hour. For absolute real-world elapsed time across different time zones, use a timezone-aware calculation instead.

Can I print or save the result?

Yes. Select the save control on a result heading to open your browser's print dialog. You can print it or choose Save as PDF if your browser offers that option.
