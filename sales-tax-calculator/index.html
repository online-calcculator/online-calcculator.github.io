How to Use the Sales Tax Calculator

Enter any two of the following: the price before tax, the sales tax rate as a percentage, or the price after tax. Leave the value you want to find empty, and click Calculate. Results appear above the form, showing the before-tax price, the sales tax rate and amount, and the tax-inclusive total.

To calculate the amount due at checkout, enter the before-tax price and rate. To remove tax from a receipt, enter the after-tax total and rate. To determine the effective rate from known prices, enter the before-tax and after-tax prices. The Clear button empties all three fields; the result panel's Save control exports a text summary.

Sales Tax Formulas

Let P be the before-tax price, R the sales tax rate expressed as a percentage, T the sales tax amount, and F the after-tax price.

  • Find the sales tax amount: T = P × R ÷ 100.
  • Find the after-tax price: F = P × (1 + R ÷ 100).
  • Find the before-tax price: P = F ÷ (1 + R ÷ 100).
  • Find the sales tax rate: R = (F ÷ P − 1) × 100, when P is greater than zero.

Worked Example

Suppose an item costs $100.00 before tax and the tax rate is 6.5%. The sales tax is $100.00 × 0.065 = $6.50. The amount due after tax is $100.00 + $6.50 = $106.50.

You can also reverse the calculation: $106.50 ÷ 1.065 = $100.00 before tax. If the before-tax and after-tax amounts are already known, ($106.50 ÷ $100.00 − 1) × 100 yields a rate of 6.5%.

Understanding the Results

Before Tax Price is the price before sales tax is added. Sale Tax shows both the percentage applied and the dollar amount charged. After Tax Price is the amount including sales tax. Dollar amounts and displayed percentage rates are formatted to two decimal places, while calculations use the full numerical precision of the entered values before formatting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove sales tax from a total?

Divide the total price by 1 plus the tax rate as a decimal. For example, at 8% tax, divide the total by 1.08. Subtract the resulting before-tax price from the total to find the tax already included.

Can I calculate the rate if I only know the two prices?

Yes. Enter the before-tax and after-tax amounts and leave the rate blank. The before-tax price must be greater than zero for this calculation, and the after-tax amount must not be lower than the before-tax amount.

Can I use this calculator for VAT or GST?

The same basic percentage arithmetic applies to simple VAT or GST price additions and reverse calculations. The calculator does not determine statutory rates, exemptions, tax credits, multiple tax tiers, or jurisdiction-specific rounding rules.

Why might a receipt differ by a cent?

Merchants and tax authorities can round tax per item, per line, or on the transaction total. This calculator computes from the supplied figures and displays monetary values to the nearest cent, so some receipts may differ slightly.

Does the calculator know the correct tax rate for my location?

No. Enter the applicable combined tax rate yourself. Rates and taxable categories can change depending on the location and the goods or services sold.
