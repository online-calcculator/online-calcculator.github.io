How to Use the Hours Calculator

Use the first calculator for two clock times when the dates are not important. Enter a start and end time using a 12-hour clock (such as 8:30 and 5:30), select AM or PM for each, and choose Calculate. The result appears above the form in hours and minutes, with the total number of minutes on a separate line.

Use Hours Between Two Dates when you know the starting and ending calendar dates. Select the month and day, enter the year, choose each time and AM/PM, and calculate. The calendar icons provide an alternative way to select a date. The Now buttons fill only their adjacent clock-time fields; the swap control applies to the first calculator.

Hours Calculation Formula

Convert each clock time to minutes since midnight: minutes = (hour mod 12) × 60 + minute + (720 if PM). Subtract start minutes from end minutes. For the first calculator, if the end time is earlier, it is treated as the following day. Identical start and end clock times give zero elapsed hours.

For two specified dates, multiply the number of intervening calendar days by 1,440, add the difference in the clock times, and report the absolute time interval. The calculator uses 24-hour civil days and does not adjust for daylight saving time transitions.

Worked Example: 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM

8:30 AM is 510 minutes after midnight, and 5:30 PM is 1,050 minutes after midnight. The difference is 1,050 − 510 = 540 minutes, which equals 9 hours. As an overnight example, 10:15 PM to 6:45 AM on the next day equals 8 hours 30 minutes or 510 minutes.

Understanding 12-Hour and 24-Hour Time

The 12-hour system distinguishes morning and afternoon using AM and PM. 12:00 AM means midnight (00:00 in 24-hour notation), while 12:00 PM means noon (12:00 in 24-hour notation). For example, 3:45 PM is 15:45 on a 24-hour clock.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if the end time is earlier than the start time?

For two clock times without dates, the calculator assumes the shift crosses midnight. For the date-based version, both dates are explicit, so the result is the absolute difference between the specified date-times.

Does the calculator count seconds or daylight saving changes?

No. Both calculators work to the nearest whole minute. The date-based calculator treats every calendar day as 24 hours rather than applying daylight saving or other time-zone adjustments.

Can I save or print the result?

Yes. Once you calculate, choose Save to download a text summary or Print to use the browser’s print or Save as PDF function.
